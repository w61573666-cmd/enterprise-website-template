HOW WE PARTNER

How We Partner

  1. Initial Enquiry

    Submit your interest via the online form, phone or WhatsApp — a dedicated account manager replies within one business day.

  2. Assessment & Proposal

    We discuss your scope and target market, then share credentials, product catalogue and a draft cooperation plan.

  3. Samples & Trial Order

    Physical samples confirm quality standards; a small trial order validates delivery and service response.

  4. Agreement & Authorization

    We sign the cooperation agreement covering pricing, territory, settlement terms and after-sales clauses.

  5. Long-term Support

    Ongoing supply assurance, new product launches, technical training and joint marketing support.
