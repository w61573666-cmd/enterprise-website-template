Quality Management System

From quarry selection to site acceptance, 68 inspection nodes span the whole chain — with capacity backup mechanisms that keep large projects on time.

QUALITY CONTROL

Most quality risks in stone projects come not from the absence of inspection, but from inspection that doesn't cover the whole chain, standards that drift, or problems that can't be traced and remedied quickly.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED operates a quality system spanning quarry, factory and site: 68 inspection nodes cover the journey from raw block selection to site acceptance, backed by a capacity redundancy mechanism that keeps large projects on schedule even under unexpected events.

Quality Control

Key Capabilities

⛏️

Quarry-Level Control

Long-term partnerships with 180+ certified quarries worldwide; block batches are pre-checked for radioactivity, physical strength and appearance before purchase — failing sources never enter our stock.

🏭

In-Process Control

Five in-line inspection nodes cover cutting, machining and protection; equipment parameters and inspection data are linked, so drift is corrected the moment it appears.

🚢

Dispatch & Arrival Checks

100% final inspection before packing; on arrival we assist owners and supervisors with re-inspection, resolving damage or color issues quickly against batch files.

🛠️

Acceptance Support

Acceptance checklists and test-report templates support a smooth owner and supervisor sign-off; after-sales queries trace back to the original quarry and batch by slab code.

Key Parameters & Standards

Inspection Points68 inspection steps across quarry, production, dispatch, arrival and installation
Inspection NodesRaw intake / cutting / machining / protection / packing / arrival re-check
StandardsQuantified criteria across dimensions, appearance, color difference, gloss and protection effect
TraceabilityUnique slab codes link quarry, batch, inspection imagery and measured data
AssuranceCapacity redundancy + multi-quarry resource pool keep supply continuous under disruption

The Three-Link Quality Chain

  1. Quarry Link

    Block pre-inspection: radioactivity, physical strength and appearance checked and filed by batch; only approved blocks ship.

  2. Factory Link

    Full-process QC: five in-line nodes + machine-vision full inspection + human re-check, with all 68 steps recorded as data.

  3. Site Link

    Delivery & acceptance: arrival re-inspection, installation guidance, checklists and report support — after-sales always has files to consult.
