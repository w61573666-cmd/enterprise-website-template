Nano Protective Coating

Matched penetrating protectors form a transparent network deep inside the stone's micro-pores — 8× stain resistance, 20 years longer service life.

NANO PROTECTION

Natural stone is full of invisible micro-pores and capillary channels. Water rings, tea stains and oil marks creep through these channels and set as stubborn stains deep inside the stone. Protection, done properly, blocks those channels without altering the stone's natural character.

Hengsheng applies a nano-scale penetrating protection system, matching protector chemistry to each stone variety and application area. Verified by third-party testing institutions, treated stone shows 8× better stain resistance and an expected service life extended by 20 years — with no visible change to colour or gloss.

Nano Protection

Key Capabilities

🔬

Nano-Scale Penetration

Protective molecules are far smaller than the stone's pores, penetrating 3–5mm below the surface to form a 3D protective network — not a surface film that wears off.

💧

Hydrophobic Yet Breathable

The layer strongly repels water and oil so spills bead up and wipe away, while remaining vapour-permeable — internal moisture escapes normally, avoiding delamination and hollowness.

🎨

Natural Look Preserved

The transparent formula leaves no visible film: colour, veining and touch stay essentially unchanged — matte stays matte, polished stays clear.

🧪

Matched to Each Stone

Different pore structures and mineral compositions across marble, granite, travertine and luxury stone call for different protector types and application methods; floors, walls and worktops each get tailored plans.

Key Parameters & Standards

Stain ResistanceThird-party verified 8× improvement against tea, coffee, red wine, cooking oil and other common stains
Expected Service LifeExtended by 20 years
Penetration Depth3–5mm below the surface (varies with porosity)
Appearance ImpactNo visible change to colour or gloss; matte stays non-reflective, polished stays clear
Application AdviceSix-face protection before installation + reinforced face treatment after; wet areas (bathrooms, kitchens) need shorter re-coating cycles

Four-Step Protection Workflow

  1. Cleaning & Drying

    Slabs are deep-cleaned and fully dried before treatment; moisture content must reach target so no water is sealed inside.

  2. Six-Face Application

    Protective agent is sprayed or roller-applied to all six faces, including edges and backs, eliminating every water-entry path.

  3. Curing

    Slabs rest 24–48 hours depending on chemistry, allowing protective molecules to penetrate and bond with the mineral structure.

  4. Effect Sampling

    Each batch is randomly sampled for beading and stain tests; protection is verified before the slabs leave the factory.
