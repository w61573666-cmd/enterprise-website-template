Careers at HSST · Global Hiring

Slab Sourcing Specialist (Türkiye/Greece Line)

Hong Kong / GuangdongFull-TimeOpenings: 2

HK$22,000–36,000 / month + sourcing performance bonus

Buy marble slabs from Turkish and Greek quarries and factories (Denizli whites, Volakas, Thassos): plant slab selection, batch negotiation, container tracking and quality-claim handling.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Maintain relationships with 15+ partner factories in Türkiye/Greece and execute quarterly sourcing plans;
  2. Select and verify slabs at origin factories, locking colour-code batches;
  3. Negotiate slab prices, cutting sizes and lead times; manage procurement cost and inventory turnover;
  4. Track booking, container loading and documentation, coordinating inspection & quarantine requirements;
  5. Handle arrival quality disputes and claims; maintain supplier quality scoring;
  6. Analyse origin-market price trends and recommend sourcing strategy.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Education: Bachelor's degree or above in international trade, materials or languages;
  • Languages: fluent English; Turkish/Greek preferred;
  • Experience: 2+ years stone procurement or marble slab trading;
  • Skills: slab quality judgement and colour-code systems; L/C and T/T flows; international logistics;
  • Attributes: strong negotiator, meticulous; able to travel to origin 3–5 times a year;
  • Others: comfortable working independently overseas.

Bonus Points

  • Existing Denizli/Volakas sourcing network preferred;
  • Arabic or another third language a plus;
  • ERP procurement module experience a plus.
