Warehouse & Logistics Supervisor (Singapore)

SingaporeFull-Time · Long-Term PostingOpenings: 1

S$3,800–5,500 / month + year-end bonus

Based at the Singapore hub, run receiving-to-dispatch flows, bulk allocation, local delivery coordination and team management — the on-site gatekeeper of the 'orders that never sleep' promise.

Key Responsibilities

  1. Manage receiving, put-away, picking and dispatch flows with inventory accuracy at the Singapore warehouse;
  2. Own slab storage safety (A-frames, vertical stacking, humidity/sun protection) and fire compliance;
  3. Coordinate sea/land container moves and local delivery drivers, controlling cost and lead time;
  4. Liaise with HK HQ and Malaysia/Indonesia clients on order allocation;
  5. Lead a 5–10 person warehouse team (incl. local workers) with scheduling and performance reviews;
  6. Run monthly stock-takes and submit inventory and operations reports.

Requirements & Qualifications

  • Education: Associate degree or above in logistics or supply chain;
  • Experience: 5+ years warehouse management incl. 2+ years as supervisor; building materials/stone preferred;
  • Languages: English for work; Mandarin for communication;
  • Skills: WMS/ERP proficiency; familiar with Singapore warehouse safety and MOM rules; forklift license preferred;
  • Attributes: strong execution, integrity and reliability;
  • Others: eligible for a Singapore work pass (company-sponsored).

Bonus Points

  • Free-trade-zone / bonded warehouse experience a plus;
  • Large fragile goods (slabs/furniture) storage experience preferred;
  • Reporting/data skills a plus.
