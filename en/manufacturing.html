Smart Manufacturing Hub

Smart Manufacturing Hub
Precision in Stone, Powered by a Digital Line

Italian five-axis CNC machining centers, fully automated polishing lines, ultra-high-pressure waterjets, and AI-driven inspection & traceability systems — Hengsheng's internationally-grade dust-free smart manufacturing base turns quarry blocks into project-ready building materials. Our processing precision reaches ±0.05mm, with a first-pass acceptance rate of ≥ 99.9% and an annual processing capacity of 2.6 million m².

Core Equipment

Italian & German–Imported Equipment Matrix

All equipment is imported from Europe's leading manufacturers and maintained on site by our production-management team (core members averaging 18+ years of industry experience), ensuring every stone's dimensions, surface finish and edge details meet international project standards.

⚙️

Italian Five-Axis CNC Center

For complex shapes, artistic medallions and high-precision carving. Per-piece repeatability within ±0.02mm. Capable of multi-curvature, chamfer and three-dimensional forms.

💧

Ultra-High-Pressure Waterjet

Precision cold cutting for glass, marble, granite and ceramic tile — cuts without thermal stress and delivers clean edges that go straight into the medallion line with no secondary grinding.

🪞

Fully Automated Polishing Line

10-head continuous polishing line — handles slabs up to 3200 × 2000mm. Each station is servo-closed-loop controlled; final gloss is held stable at 90 ± 5 GSL.

🪚

CNC Infrared Bridge Saw

For fixed-size cutting and grooving; both length- and cross-cuts plus chamfering in a single station. Max cutting thickness 80mm, tolerance ±0.1mm.

🧊

Automated Three-Dimensional Warehouse

Capacity for 80,000+ m² of ready-stock slabs. Each SKU / batch / vein-direction is precisely indexed, and standard-sample quote turnaround is ≤ 12 hours.

📷

AI Surface-Defect Vision Inspection

Every slab is automatically scanned by machine vision before shipping; microscopic defects — cracks, voids, color drift, vein breakage — are detected and auto-graded.

Quality System

68 Quality Inspection Nodes · Full-Chain Traceability from Quarry to Site

We decompose inspection into seven layers — Quarry Screening → Incoming Sampling → First-Slab Inspection → In-Process Inspection → Pre-Medallion Re-Inspection → Pre-Pack Final Inspection → Outbound Random Sampling — comprising 68 concrete nodes. Every shipment ships with a third-party test report.

01Quarry ScreeningRadionuclide limits; Class-A decorative-material standard
02Incoming SamplingWater absorption, compressive strength, dimensional tolerance, surface defects
03First-Slab InspectionVein matching, color-difference grading, thickness tolerance
04In-Process Inspection100% intermediate inspection on every completed process step
05Pre-Medallion Re-InspectionPre-assembly scan; further color-difference grading
06Pre-Pack Final InspectionGloss, flatness, chamfer, code cross-check
07Outbound Random SamplingAQL 1.0 sampling with full-chain image & data capture
Craft & Customization

220 Proprietary Processes · From Standard Product to Work of Art

Beyond standard slabs, medallion plates and carved panels, the proprietary processes we co-developed with Italian master craftsmen span four major categories — Surface Treatment, Edge Profile, Special Joint, and Artistic Decoration — available either as tailored designs for your project or applied from our existing process library.

Surface Treatment

  • Polished / Honed
  • Acid-wash / Antique
  • Brushed / Sandblasted
  • Water-jet / Flamed

Edge Profile

  • Straight / Beveled / Bullnose / Dupont
  • Chamfer / Groove / Carved Edge
  • Waterjet Joint Cutting (≤ 0.5mm)

Special Joint

  • Book-match
  • Vein-match
  • Random-match
  • Mosaic

Artistic Decoration

  • Waterjet Medallion
  • Relief / Openwork / Three-Dimensional Carving
  • Full-Slab Custom Luxury-Stone Feature Walls
