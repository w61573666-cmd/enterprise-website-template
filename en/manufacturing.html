Smart Manufacturing Hub

Smart Manufacturing Hub
Precision in Stone, Powered by a Digital Line

Italian five-axis CNC machining centers, fully automated polishing lines, ultra-high-pressure waterjets, and AI-driven inspection & traceability systems — Hengsheng's internationally-grade dust-free smart manufacturing base turns quarry blocks into project-ready building materials. Our processing precision reaches ±0.05mm, with a first-pass acceptance rate of ≥ 99.9% and an annual processing capacity of 2.6 million m².

Core Equipment

Italian & German–Imported Equipment Matrix

All equipment is imported from Europe's leading manufacturers and maintained on site by our production-management team (core members averaging 18+ years of industry experience), ensuring every stone's dimensions, surface finish and edge details meet international project standards.

⚙️

Italian Five-Axis CNC Center

For complex shapes, artistic medallions and high-precision carving. Per-piece repeatability within ±0.02mm. Capable of multi-curvature, chamfer and three-dimensional forms.

💧

Ultra-High-Pressure Waterjet

Precision cold cutting for glass, marble, granite and ceramic tile — cuts without thermal stress and delivers clean edges that go straight into the medallion line with no secondary grinding.

🪞

Fully Automated Polishing Line

10-head continuous polishing line — handles slabs up to 3200 × 2000mm. Each station is servo-closed-loop controlled; final gloss is held stable at 90 ± 5 GSL.

🪚

CNC Infrared Bridge Saw

For fixed-size cutting and grooving; both length- and cross-cuts plus chamfering in a single station. Max cutting thickness 80mm, tolerance ±0.1mm.

🧊

Automated Three-Dimensional Warehouse

Capacity for 80,000+ m² of ready-stock slabs. Each SKU / batch / vein-direction is precisely indexed, and standard-sample quote turnaround is ≤ 12 hours.

📷

AI Surface-Defect Vision Inspection

Every slab is automatically scanned by machine vision before shipping; microscopic defects — cracks, voids, color drift, vein breakage — are detected and auto-graded.

Full Production Flow

From Block to Site · 8-Step Production Flow

Every step maps to dedicated quality-inspection nodes (68 in total) and digital records — processes are auditable and responsibilities traceable.

01

Block Procurement & Grading

Direct sourcing from 60+ countries including Italy, Turkey and Greece; colour grading and hidden-crack testing before intake.

02

Layout & Numbering

Slabs from each block are laid out and numbered by veining; shade direction locked for large surfaces in advance.

03

Gang / Wire-Saw Cutting

Italian gang saws cut slabs with thickness tolerance within ±0.5mm.

04

Automated Polishing Line

German automated line delivers gloss ≥ 90°, with honed / flamed finishes switched on demand.

05

CNC Bridge-Saw Cut-to-Size

CNC bridge saws cut to drawings; each piece checked for dimensions and diagonal deviation.

06

5-Axis CNC & Waterjet

5-axis CNC and waterjet centers produce specials, medallions and carvings at ±0.05mm.

07

Protection & Final Inspection

Six-face nano protection applied; the final 12 of 68 inspection nodes release one by one.

08

Packing & Loading

Four-layer packing (custom crates + moisture film + cushioning), numbering verified against packing lists before container loading.

FAQ

Manufacturing Hub FAQ

QDo you accept toll processing of customer-owned blocks? How is the material safeguarded?

Yes. Customer blocks are photographed and numbered on intake with dedicated batch management; processing is physically separated from our own production. Delivery includes processing records and offcut lists per batch, and customers are welcome to witness container loading.

QWhere exactly does the "300% efficiency leap" show up?

After the 2026 integration of 5-axis CNC with digital scheduling: scheduling response cut from 48 hours to 6 hours, equipment idle time down 40%, and specials delivery time down 40% — consolidated as a 300% efficiency leap.

QCan we visit the factory?

Yes. Our Guangdong base welcomes customer visits by appointment — see the equipment matrix, the 68 inspection nodes in operation and current batches. Overseas customers can book a live video tour hosted by production leads.

QHow is batch shading kept within 3 grades for bulk supply?

Triple assurance: same-mining-section block selection at the quarry stage; fully digital layout simulation at the layout stage; and batch-consistency comparison among the 12 final checks — any failed batch is withheld and re-matched.
