HSST Certified as Hong Kong Preferred Green Building-Material Supplier — Green Line Reaches 45%

Home › News › HSST Certified as Hong Kong …

On 28 August 2026, HSST passed the rigorous joint assessment by the Hong Kong Green Building Council and third-party auditors, earning the 'Preferred Green Building-Material Supplier' certification across 62 indicators in four dimensions, with an overall Grade-A score.

HSST's green product line now stands at 45% of revenue, spanning recycled-aggregate composites, low-energy sintered stone and EPD-declared imported blocks. The certification has placed HSST on the green-procurement lists of 12 leading developers.

The Hard Power Behind Green Certification

The certification was jointly assessed by the Hong Kong Green Building Council and third-party auditors across 62 indicators in four dimensions: raw-material traceability, production energy use, carbon footprint and logistics. HSST's green product share has climbed from 28% in 2024 to 45%, covering recycled-aggregate composites, low-energy sintered stone and EPD-declared imported blocks.

Industry data puts green stone's market share above 40% in 2026, and 'quality housing' policies are rapidly lifting demand for dry-hang stone facades. HSST built its green product matrix two years ahead of the curve — green certification is no longer a bonus point, but the entry ticket to top developers' procurement lists.
