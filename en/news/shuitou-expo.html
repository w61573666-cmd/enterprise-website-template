25th China Shuitou International Stone Fair Announced for November 2026

Home › News › 25th China Shuitou Internati…

The 25th China Shuitou International Stone Fair returns November 8-11, 2026 with a record 85,000 sqm across 2,000 exhibitors and 153,000+ visitors. As China's largest and most influential stone industry event, it covers stone blocks, slabs, tiles, crafts, and processing machinery from around the world.

85,000 m² of Industry Direction

Shuitou is China's largest stone hub, and this edition's 2,000 exhibitors and 153,000 expected visitors set records again. The 2026 subtext is transformation: low-end grey-slab lines are shutting while white and light-luxury stone runs at full capacity, and leading exhibitors' stands grew 30%.

HSST exhibits for the 11th year, highlighting custom shaped work and the Hong Kong stock system — its differentiator here is 'Hong Kong delivery standard': batch locking, sealed samples, 48-hour replenishment.
