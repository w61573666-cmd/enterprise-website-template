Ahead of the EU Carbon Label Deadline: HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Partners with Italy's CARBOTEC for Full-Range LCA

Home › News › Ahead of the EU Carbon Label…

In October 2025 HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED formally partnered with Italy's CARBOTEC laboratory to run life-cycle assessments (LCA) to EN 15804 across its full product range, preparing 18 months ahead of the EU Construction Products Carbon Footprint rules that become mandatory on 1 January 2027.

EPD declarations for the first six flagship varieties are due in Q4 2026. With water recycling, rooftop solar and short-haul logistics, HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED's carbon intensity is projected 30% below industry average — a direct purchasing advantage in Europe.

18 Months Ahead of the Regulation

The EU Construction Products Carbon Footprint rules become mandatory on 1 January 2027, requiring certified carbon labels on all construction stone (HS 6801/6802) entering the EU. Partnering with Italy's CARBOTEC laboratory, HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED is running full life-cycle assessments (LCA) to EN 15804 across its product range.

EPD declarations for the first six flagship varieties are due in Q4 2026. Thanks to water-recycling, rooftop solar and short-haul logistics, HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED's carbon intensity is projected 30% below industry average — a direct purchasing advantage with European clients.
