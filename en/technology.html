FAQ

Technical FAQ

QWhat does ±0.05mm CNC precision mean on site?

For stone, ±0.05mm means curtain-wall panels install without secondary trimming, medallion joints close to ≤ 0.3mm, and joints on bevelled or shaped components are invisible from three metres. It is the foundation of the finish quality demanded by five-star hotels and landmarks.

QDoes nano protection change the stone's appearance, and how often is it reapplied?

Penetrating nano protection forms a film inside the pores — no plastic gloss on the surface; matte and polished finishes keep their original feel. In standard indoor environments the protection lasts 5 years; wet and high-traffic zones should be re-treated every 3 years. We provide re-application plans and site service.

QCan smart inspection provide traceable records for every batch?

Yes. Every cut-to-size piece carries a unique number linked to its quarry batch, machining equipment, inspection imagery and reviewer. Delivery includes an electronic quality dossier — owners and supervisors can verify piece by piece, and tenders or audits can cite it directly.

QWon't 68 inspection nodes slow down delivery?

Quite the opposite. Front-loaded quality interception sharply reduces rework, and with the 2026 smart-factory scheduling integration, overall efficiency is up 300% — standard bulk deliveries run 20% – 30% faster than industry norms.
