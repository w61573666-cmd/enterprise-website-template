Sustainable Mining

Mine & Remediate in Parallel — Long-Term Symbiosis with Earth

Natural stone is a precious, non-renewable gift of the Earth's geological evolution. We require every partner quarry to hold the ISO 14001 Environmental Management System certification and to operate a "mine-and-remediate" sustainable-mining model: for every 1 m³ of block extracted, we restore 1.2 m³ of topsoil and re-establish vegetation in parallel; mine-site drainage is 100% settled and recycled; quarry waste is classified into three streams — construction aggregate / engineered-stone feedstock / road-base material — for recovery.

Supply Assurance

Four-Layer Supply Assurance System

We lock the three biggest risks of project supply — stock-outs, price spikes and shade drift — within a predictable range.

① Direct Quarry Contracts & Exclusives

Core varieties are contracted directly with quarry districts (e.g. the 10-year Carrara exclusive), bypassing middle traders for both price and supply stability.

② Dual / Multi-Source Backup

Every flagship variety has 2 – 3 backup quarries; if one district stops for weather, permitting or geology, we switch within 48 hours.

③ Strategic Inventory

Hong Kong and Guangdong warehouses keep 80,000 m²+ of slab stock across 60+ flagship varieties; urgent orders ship from stock in as little as 7 days to Hong Kong.

④ Batch Reservation

Large projects reserve block batches and slab numbers at kickoff, supplying from the same batch number throughout to eliminate mid-project shade drift.

FAQ

Sourcing & Supply FAQ

QWhat does "60+ countries, 180+ quarries" mean exactly?

60+ countries is the coverage of block origin countries; 180+ is the number of partner quarries that passed all three diligence rounds (geological stability, production stability, ESG compliance) and remain actively supplying. The list is reviewed annually — underperformers are removed, new ones added.

QCan we reserve blocks with a specific colour band or veining?

Yes. For luxury and rare varieties we offer block-selection reservation: visit the quarry to choose in person, or have our resident consultant select remotely with 4K video. Reserved blocks are sealed and shipped as a dedicated batch.

QHow should the 1 : 1.2 "mine-and-remediate" figure be read?

For every 1 m³ of block extracted, partner quarries must complete 1.2 m³ of topsoil backfill and vegetation restoration on the worked face. It is a hard ESG requirement for all partner quarries and an annual audit item.

QWhat is the process for developing a new quarry source?

Four steps: geological & variety pre-assessment → three diligence rounds (geology / capacity / ESG) → trial orders validating processing performance and batch stability → long-term agreement joining the supply network. The full cycle typically takes 6 – 12 months, ensuring a new quarry meets Hengsheng standards from day one.

Need a Specific Source or Rare Variety?

Our quarry team can recommend blocks from a specific country, seam or variety based on your project. We can also advise on the appropriate thickness and surface treatment for your climate, location and use case. Reach out to the quarry team directly.
