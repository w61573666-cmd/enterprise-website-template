Global Quarries
60+ Countries · 180+ Quarries · 300+ Varieties

From Italy's Carrara to Brazil's Minas Gerais, from Turkey's Afyon to India's Rajasthan — Hengsheng's global direct-sourcing network took 27+ years to build into a resource system that is hard to replicate. Every partner quarry is selected through three diligence rounds (geological stability, production stability, ESG compliance). All extraction plans strictly follow international environmental standards and a "mine-and-remediate" sustainable model.

Sustainable Mining

Mine & Remediate in Parallel — Long-Term Symbiosis with Earth

Natural stone is a precious, non-renewable gift of the Earth's geological evolution. We require every partner quarry to hold the ISO 14001 Environmental Management System certification and to operate a "mine-and-remediate" sustainable-mining model: for every 1 m³ of block extracted, we restore 1.2 m³ of topsoil and re-establish vegetation in parallel; mine-site drainage is 100% settled and recycled; quarry waste is classified into three streams — construction aggregate / engineered-stone feedstock / road-base material — for recovery.
