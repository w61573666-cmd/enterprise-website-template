FAQ

Recruiting FAQ

QCan I apply without a stone-industry background?

Yes. Design, commercial and digital-operations roles prioritize transferable skills; a full industry-knowledge onboarding and mentoring programme is provided. Specialist roles such as quarry consultant or CNC programmer do require relevant technical backgrounds.

QWhat support is offered for overseas postings (e.g. Italy)?

Overseas allowance, housing support, annual flights home, local medical insurance and family-visit arrangements. Language training continues before and after deployment.

QHow long does the hiring process take?

Typically 2 – 3 weeks from application to offer: screening within 7 working days, 1 – 2 professional interviews, a senior panel for management roles, and offers issued within 3 working days after the final round.

QIf the company is not listed and not a group, is growth limited?

We are a 27+ year Hong Kong private enterprise — 4,200+ projects and a delivery network across 60+ countries are the proof of scope. Skipping capital-market manoeuvres keeps decisions long-term and promotions performance-based; that is precisely the strength of a slow-built company.

How to Apply

How to Join Us

  1. Step 1 · Send Your CV: email your bilingual CV and portfolio (if applicable) to careers@hsst.hk with the subject line "Name — Position — Preferred Location".
  2. Step 2 · Screening Interview: HR responds within 7 working days. Successful candidates proceed to 1–2 rounds of professional interviews plus an HR interview.
  3. Step 3 · Comprehensive Review: management or core roles include a comprehensive panel with Hengsheng's core decision-makers.
  4. Step 4 · Offer & Onboarding: offers are issued within 3 working days. Pre-employment documentation includes a medical check, education verification and a clean criminal-record certificate.
