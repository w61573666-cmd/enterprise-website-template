Build Stone Into a Life-Long Profession

We're looking for people with genuine passion for quarries, processing, projects, design and global trade — whether you're a second-generation stone professional, a technical specialist from mining engineering, or a craftsperson ready to translate an architect's design language into finished product. At Hengsheng, colleagues averaging 18+ years of industry experience mentor you, and 27+ years of accumulated global network back you up.

Why Join Hengsheng

Treat Stone as a Profession, Not a Transaction

Real Global Network

Our work spans the Hong Kong office, the Carrara quarries in Italy, the Guangdong processing base, and landmark project sites in Dubai. Daily collaboration with people from many countries — a genuinely cross-border, cross-cultural experience.

Long-Term Career Platform

27+ years of cumulative experience, 4,200+ delivered projects, and a long-term customer-renewal rate consistently above 85% — a slow company, but one where you have time to do things properly.

Mature Mentorship

Core team members average 18+ years of industry experience. New hires receive 1:1 mentorship from senior colleagues, with study-trip opportunities to stone-industry hubs in Italy and Germany.

Competitive Compensation

Industry-competitive base salary + performance bonus + a comprehensive benefits package — medical coverage, paid leave, family-care leave and an employee equity pool.

How to Apply

How to Join Us

  1. Step 1 · Send Your CV: email your bilingual CV and portfolio (if applicable) to careers@hsst.hk with the subject line "Name — Position — Preferred Location".
  2. Step 2 · Screening Interview: HR responds within 7 working days. Successful candidates proceed to 1–2 rounds of professional interviews plus an HR interview.
  3. Step 3 · Comprehensive Review: management or core roles include a comprehensive panel with Hengsheng's core decision-makers.
  4. Step 4 · Offer & Onboarding: offers are issued within 3 working days. Pre-employment documentation includes a medical check, education verification and a clean criminal-record certificate.
