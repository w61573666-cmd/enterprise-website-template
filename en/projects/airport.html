Design Concept · Balancing Passenger Efficiency and Enduring Materials

Terminal 3 is conceived as "flowing cloud": the check-in hall takes warm Botticino as its feature wall, giving travellers a sense of welcome; the security lanes, boarding bridges and transfer lobby use Silver Grey Granite for visual unity, noise reduction and wear resistance; the VIP and first-class lounges use book-matched Statuario slabs for ceremony — three stone vocabularies carrying passengers from arrival to boarding.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone supplied and installed all public-area stone: 55,000 ㎡ of walls and floors, 3,800 round columns, 12,000 floor pieces and 2,400 tactile-paving segments, all factory-numbered and five-axis CNC machined to ±0.5 mm, then trucked at night direct to 64 island work zones — the airport never closed, with just 4.5 hours of work each night across 18 months.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from 3D scanning to island-by-island night installation — T3 never closed; every noisy trade fitted into the 4.5-hour night window.

01

3D Scanning

360° laser scanning of the 64 island work zones produced millimetre-level point clouds; BIM overlay resolved 412 structural deviations in advance.

02

Island Paving

All floor slabs were numbered and crated in the factory by work island, trucked at night direct to their island and assembled in a single shift.

03

Heavy Lifting

Curved panels for 3,800 round columns lifted with vacuum suction and purpose-made slings, five-axis CNC pre-machined to ±0.5 mm.

04

Island Inspection

Every batch passed 100% inspection at a temporary inspection island before entering a work zone; anything failing went back to the factory — no delayed rework.

05

Column Cladding

The 8.4 m columns were clad in pre-cast segments, assembled in place with joints held under 0.8 mm.

06

Night Works

23:30–04:00 was the only work window; all noisy trades concentrated there. Across 18 months the airport never stopped operating.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — at the world's busiest airport, stone quality hides in the millimetres.

Underbolt Dry-hanging

The 12 m double-height walls use underbolt dry-hanging with three-way adjustable clamps; 304 stainless fittings passed 2 million fatigue cycles.

Joint Control

Standard 1.5 mm joints with color-matched epoxy read visually as ≤0.3 mm after polishing; the security zone's year-round vibration causes no cracking.

Stair Anti-slip

Passenger stair treads use double-groove anti-slip strips at R11 — the highest EN 16165 standard for public spaces.

Crystallised Polishing

Duty-free stone siliconised and crystallised to stain resistance grade 5; gloss still holds at 90° after 24 hours of foot traffic.

Book-match Pre-assembly

Twenty-four Statuario slabs pre-assembled and approved in the factory; all four VIP passages assembled on site with zero errors.

Movement Joints

For the terminal's 60 ℃ temperature range, expansion joints sealed with elastic sealant all passed 200,000 fatigue cycles.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

The world's densest passenger flows and delivery without closure — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers with data.

👣
CHALLENGE 01

Wear and slip double control across 55,000 ㎡

T3 handles 230,000 passengers a day; the floors must be both mirror-beautiful and slip-safe — conventional polished finishes cannot do both.

Solution: Public flooring uses flamed Silver Grey Granite throughout, R11 slip resistance UKAS-tested; 18 months of heavy traffic and not one slip incident.
🛫
CHALLENGE 02

4.5-hour night windows, airport open

T3 operated throughout; the legal work window was tiny, so every noisy trade had to fit into 23:30–04:00.

Solution: Pre-planned island-by-island night installation with pre-cast components and factory staging 72 hours ahead; 540 night windows completed over 18 months with zero flight delays.
📏
CHALLENGE 03

Airport-grade flatness and dynamic-load fatigue

The terminal sees 45 ℃ seasonal swings and over 100,000 crowd-load cycles a day; ordinary floors quickly debond and crack.

Solution: Every slab factory pre-assembled and numbered to ±1.5 mm, laid to number and re-levelled on site; after 200,000 fatigue cycles, no cracking five years on.
🧰
CHALLENGE 04

60+ equipment interfaces, fully resolved

Check-in islands, security lanes, boarding bridges and smart baggage systems bring 60-plus equipment interfaces meeting the stone.

Solution: BIM detailing produced 56 junction details with factory pre-cast metal-and-stone composite pieces; on-site interface rework ran at 0.4% against an industry average of 5%.
