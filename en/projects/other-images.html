PROJECT ARCHIVE

Other Project Images

A long-term archive of HENGSHENG's completed project photography and raw slab samples. The photographs record how different stones perform once laid, dry-hung and finished in real spaces, while the slab samples show the true ground colour, vein direction and slab grade of each variety — reference material for designers, contractors and owners during material selection. The archive spans marble, granite and limestone installations across hotels, retail, residential and public works, plus close-up slab photography of Calacatta Gold, Nero Marquina, Crema Marfil and other signature varieties.
