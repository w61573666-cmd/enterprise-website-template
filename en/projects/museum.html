Design Concept · Conservation and Aesthetics in Museum Stone

The city museum is built around "conservation and aesthetics": Carrara White large slab hanging walls for the 8 themed galleries provide the neutral backdrop for artifacts, Calacatta Gold for the lecture auditorium and collection storage adds ceremony, and Crema Marfil for the public lobby and cafe provides warmth — three principal stones balance "conservation" and "public aesthetics" across the cultural architecture.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone supplied and installed all stone for the 17,000 ㎡ across 6 floors: 8,000 ㎡ of 12 mm gallery hanging walls, 4,000 ㎡ of public lobby and corridor flooring, 3,000 ㎡ of academic zone and collection storage, and 2,000 ㎡ of façade and outdoor plaza. Delivered in phased handovers over 30 months while galleries stayed closed sequentially — climate-controlled environment preserved artifact safety throughout.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from 3D scanning to phased handover — 30 months, gallery by gallery, climate-controlled throughout.

01

3D Scanning

360° laser scanning of the 6-floor 17,000 ㎡ museum produced millimetre-level point clouds, resolving 312 structural deviations and HVAC interface points in advance.

02

Digital Layout

8,000 ㎡ of 12 mm Carrara thin slabs pre-assembled and numbered gallery by gallery, producing piece-by-piece installation drawings aligned with the gallery-by-gallery program.

03

Factory Fabrication

Five-axis CNC machined 8,000 ㎡ of 12 mm Carrara thin slabs and 12,000 linear metres of hanging-wall substructure to a tolerance of ±0.3 mm — the higher precision demanded by thin panels.

04

Museum-grade Packing

Carrara 12 mm thin panels travelled in full-foam vacuum wooden crates; transport held a constant 18 °C and 50 % RH, with zero breakage and zero deformation.

05

Gallery Hanging Installation

Thin panels hung on back-bolts plus three-way adjustable aluminium T-brackets, capacity ≥4 kN per point; single-panel hanging time within 4 minutes to keep gallery closure short.

06

HVAC Coordination

Stone installation was BIM-coordinated in 3D with HVAC ducts, lighting track and display-case embedments; 218 service crossings resolved in advance, rework rate ≤3 %.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — in a museum, precision hides in the millimetres, the climate and the safety.

Hanging Craftwork

12 mm Carrara thin panels on M8 stainless-steel back-bolts plus aluminium T-brackets, three-way adjustable, capacity ≥4 kN per point; single-panel install time ≤4 minutes.

Thermal Insulation

Gallery stone backed with a 50 mm insulation layer plus low-conductivity silicone gaskets, preventing HVAC condensation from reaching the stone; no condensation across the gallery's ±1 °C range.

Humidity Control

Gallery stone with six-sided waterproof backing plus elastic joint sealant; no surface condensation or mould within the gallery's 50±5 % RH range.

Lighting Coordination

Gallery hanging stone, track lights and ceiling spots BIM-coordinated in 3D; light sources ≥300 mm from the stone surface, UV filtration ≥99 % to prevent artifact fading.

Anti-seismic Frame

Gallery hanging stone backed by a 50×50 mm galvanised-steel anti-seismic frame, rated for seismic intensity 8; one panel loosening does not affect its neighbors — artifacts stay safe.

Hidden Anchor

Gallery hanging stone M8 stainless-steel back-bolt 8 mm embedment, bracket three-way adjustable ±5 mm, ensuring flatness ±0.5 mm/3 m across the 8,000 ㎡ of gallery walls.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

Museum-grade climate control (±1 °C, 50±5 % RH), 12 mm thin-panel hanging craftsmanship, and 30 months of phased delivery — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers with data.

🌡️
CHALLENGE 01

Museum-grade climate control

Museum galleries require constant ±1 °C temperature and 50±5 % RH humidity; ordinary stone easily produces condensation, mould and deformation under thermal-humidity cycling, threatening artifacts.

Solution:Carrara 12 mm thin panels backed with a 50 mm insulation layer plus six-sided waterproof backing; no condensation or mould within the gallery's ±1 °C and 50±5 % RH range; humidity stayed at 50±3 % RH after five years in service.
🎨
CHALLENGE 02

12 mm thin-panel hanging craftsmanship

Museum gallery hanging walls must balance flatness, self-weight and artifact safety; ordinary 18 mm panels are heavy and warp-prone, ordinary 6 mm panels are brittle.

Solution:12 mm Carrara thin panels on M8 stainless-steel back-bolts plus three-way adjustable aluminium T-brackets, capacity ≥4 kN per point; flatness ±0.5 mm/3 m across the 8,000 ㎡ of gallery walls.
⏰
CHALLENGE 03

30 months of gallery-by-gallery delivery

8 themed galleries closed sequentially, each closed no longer than 90 days; construction must maintain artifact-conservation-grade environment throughout.

Solution:8 galleries rotated through closure, each within an 85-day construction window; gallery climate-controlled environment uninterrupted during construction; all 8 galleries handed over across 30 months, zero artifact damage.
💡
CHALLENGE 04

Lighting and conservation coordination

Gallery lighting greatly affects artifact fading; ordinary stone has high reflectance and UV transmittance, easily accelerating artifact ageing.

Solution:Gallery hanging stone, track lights and ceiling spots BIM-coordinated in 3D; light sources ≥300 mm from the stone surface, UV filtration ≥99 %; honed stone reflectance ≤40 % to prevent glare and artifact fading.
