Design Concept · Adaptable, Ceremonial Convention Stone

The international convention center is built around "column-free space": the 60 m-span halls use Silver Grey Granite large-format flooring to carry heavy exhibition loads, the VIP and conference areas use warm Botticino for a sense of occasion, and the press center and multi-function hall use contrasting Nero Marquina for depth — three principal stones balancing "adaptability" and "ceremony" in convention architecture.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone supplied and installed all stone for the 48,000 ㎡ of walls and floors and ancillary facilities: 32,000 ㎡ of long-span exhibition flooring, 16,000 ㎡ of public-area wall cladding and all VIP and back-of-house stone; delivered zone by zone over 28 months while the center kept operating, hosting 150+ events a year with zero disruption.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from 3D scanning to zoned handover — 28 months, zone by zone, while the center stayed open.

01

3D Scanning

360° laser scanning of the four 60 m-span halls produced millimetre-level point clouds, resolving 326 structural deviations in advance.

02

Digital Layout

32,000 ㎡ of slabs pre-assembled and numbered hall by hall, producing piece-by-piece installation drawings aligned with the zoned works program.

03

Factory Fabrication

Five-axis CNC machined 26,000 ㎡ of 30 mm bush-hammered granite to a tolerance of ±0.5 mm.

04

Heavy-duty Packing

The halls' 30 mm slabs travelled in steel-framed wooden crates — zero breakage throughout city delivery.

05

On-site Installation

A 50-tonne mobile crane with vacuum lifters, working nights to the zoned program so daytime events were never disturbed.

06

Movement Joints

Expansion joints at 6 m centers across the long-span halls, sealed with elastic sealant for a service life beyond 50 years.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — in a long-span venue, precision hides in the millimetres.

Movement Joints

Joints at 6 m centers in the 60 m-span halls with elastic sealant; no cracking after 100,000 fatigue cycles.

Crystallised Polishing

Hall floors siliconised and crystallised to a gloss of 90° or above, stain resistance grade 5; zero damage across 150+ events a year.

Long-span, Column-free

Hall flooring uses prestressed composite panels with stone facing, cutting self-weight by 35%.

Underfloor Cable Trenches

200×200 mm cable trenches precast into the hall floor; trench covers level with the stone to ±0.5 mm for rapid booth hook-ups.

Event Staging

Modular standard stage design with pre-set positioning inserts — a 1,000 ㎡ stage erected within 3 hours.

Maintenance

Deep cleaning within 6 hours after each event and a full re-grind each quarter; gloss still at 88° after three years in service.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

A 60 m column-free span, 150+ events a year, and construction without closing the venue — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers with data.

🏛️
CHALLENGE 01

Flatness across a 60 m column-free hall

60 m column-free structures are highly sensitive to floor flatness; conventional stone flooring easily sags mid-span or debonds.

Solution: 30 mm bush-hammered granite on prestressed composite panels cut self-weight by 35%; movement joints at 6 m centers keep mid-span flatness within ±1.5 mm — no debonding after three years.
🎪
CHALLENGE 02

Wear resistance for 150+ events a year

Hall floors host 150+ exhibitions a year; constant booth assembly and dismantling wears the surface dull.

Solution: Bush-hammered granite with 18 MPa flexural strength and grade-5 stain resistance after siliconised crystallisation; quarterly re-grinding keeps gloss at 88° after three years.
⏰
CHALLENGE 03

28 months of zoned delivery, venue open

The center kept operating throughout; works could not disturb live events, and noisy trades had to fit into gaps between exhibitions.

Solution: Works were zoned across four halls, with noisy trades concentrated between 22:00 and 06:00; 150+ events a year ran with zero disruption across all 28 months.
⚡
CHALLENGE 04

Safety under 50 kPa booth loads

Exhibition booths are equipment-dense, with average floor loads of 50 kPa; ordinary stone flooring cracks or settles under them.

Solution: 30 mm bush-hammered granite over 50 mm prestressed composite panels gives a load capacity of 100 kPa; maximum deformation ≤0.3 mm after three years.
