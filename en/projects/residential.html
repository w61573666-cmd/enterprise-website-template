Design Concept · Stone That Feels Like Home

The estate takes "mountain and water entering the home" as its theme: foyer and living rooms open with light-catching Calacatta Gold book-matched walls, bedrooms, tea rooms and corridors take Carrara White for a tranquil keynote, and courtyards and exterior walls choose warm Travertine to echo the hillside — stone becomes part of the memory of living here, not merely decoration.

HSST Stone provides whole-home bespoke service: 18,600 ㎡ of walls and floors, 96 bathrooms, 64 fireplaces and 32 staircases, each home with its own dedicated file; factory pre-assembly and numbering before a coordinated delivery, with an installation crew stationed per home right through to handover.

Construction

Construction Process

A six-stage record from site re-measure to room-by-room acceptance — one file per home, traceable end to end.

01

Site Measuring

Designer, owner and contractor re-measured on site together; laser scanning produced 1:1 point clouds, resolving 186 structural deviations across the 32 villas.

02

Selection & Layout

A dedicated selection booklet per home; slabs virtually pre-assembled, and cutting began only after the owner confirmed the book-match effect on their phone.

03

Bespoke Fabrication

Fireplaces, staircases and moulded profiles combined five-axis CNC with hand craft; curved panels machined to ±0.5 mm.

04

Numbered Packing

Crated per home and packed in installation order with foam plus wooden crates — zero breakage throughout city delivery.

05

On-site Installation

A dedicated crew per home stayed on site, coordinating daily with M&E and joinery; each villa installed in 12 days.

06

Final Finishing

Full-area grinding and crystallisation followed by room-by-room acceptance: flatness ±1.0 mm and 100% uniform joints — move-in ready.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft standards up close — luxury lives in the details a family touches every day.

Dry-hanging System

Living-room double-height walls use underbolt dry-hanging with three-way adjustable clamps that accommodate slight settlement in villa structures; concealed works carry a lifetime warranty.

Herringbone Laying

Master-bedroom corridors use herringbone marble; laser setting-out carried the 45° pattern through the home at 1.0 mm joints.

Waterjet Medallion

The foyer's 3.6 m medallion was waterjet-cut into 626 pieces, pre-assembled and numbered as a whole, then reassembled on site to the drawing.

Bespoke Fireplaces

64 marble fireplaces CNC-carved as one piece, with heat-resistant stone lining the firebox — seamless, no joints.

Book-match Joints

Book-matched wall joints tinted with colour-matched epoxy; after mirror polishing the veining crosses the joints seamlessly.

Crystallised Polishing

Whole-home stone siliconised and crystallised to grade 5 stain resistance; wet areas additionally anti-slip treated to R10 for family safety.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

Four challenges of building 32 villas side by side — HSST Stone answers with data.

📐
CHALLENGE 01

Millimetre re-measuring for whole-home bespoke

Structural and M&E deviations compound in villas, and bespoke pieces such as fireplaces and staircases are extremely dimension-sensitive.

Solution: 3D laser scanning created one file per home; all 186 deviations were resolved at the factory, leaving zero re-cutting on site.
🧱
CHALLENGE 02

Safety of cantilevers and bespoke pieces

Stair treads and fireplace cantilevers carry complex loads, and a family setting demands large safety margins.

Solution: Every bespoke piece comes with structural calculations, fixed with both underbolts and chemical anchors, all verified by a third-party structural review.
🕰️
CHALLENGE 03

9 months across 32 villas in parallel

Multiple villas under construction at once, crossing with M&E, joinery and soft furnishings — complex schedule coupling.

Solution: One dedicated crew per home with daily interface coordination meetings; factory pre-assembly cut on-site time by 40%, all 32 villas delivered on time in 9 months.
🌿
CHALLENGE 04

Home-grade environmental and anti-slip standards

Family living demands far higher standards of radioactivity and wet-area slip resistance than public buildings.

Solution: All stone supplied with Class A radioactivity reports; wet areas reach R10 slip resistance; after 48 hours of ventilation, indoor air was tested before handover.
