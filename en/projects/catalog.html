FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About the Catalogue

QHow do I obtain case dossiers similar to my project?

Tell us your project type, region, building scale or preferred varieties. Our project-archive team will retrieve and deliver briefs (PDF) of the 3 – 5 most similar projects within one working day, including varieties applied, quantities, craft highlights and site photos.

QHow are figures such as 4,200+ projects and 1,000+ hotels counted?

Based on delivery records since our founding in 1999, counted as one contract = one project across Hong Kong and 60+ countries worldwide; hotels are counted when star-rated or positioned as premium boutique. Figures are reviewed and updated annually.

QCan we visit completed representative projects?

With the owner's consent, we can arrange site visits to completed projects in Hong Kong, or provide full photo sets and videos of overseas projects with video-conference walkthroughs of craft details.

QWhat supporting documents are available for tenders?

We provide a full tender pack: company registration documents, ISO 9001 / 14001 / 45001 certificates, CE and GREENGUARD Gold certifications, Class-A radioactivity test reports, reference-project evidence and client reference letters.

Looking for Representative Projects in a Specific Scenario?

Our project-archive team can search by scenario, region, variety or scale and deliver full project dossiers (PDF briefs, specifications, products applied).
