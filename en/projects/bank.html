Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from 3D scanning to phased handover — 18 months, the HQ and 52 branches trading throughout.

01

3D Scanning

360° laser scanning of the 28-floor 12,000 ㎡ HQ and 52 branches produced millimetre-level point clouds, resolving 218 structural deviations and security interface points in advance.

02

Digital Layout

4,800 ㎡ of Statuario slabs pre-assembled and numbered floor by floor, producing piece-by-piece installation drawings aligned with the floor-by-floor program; 52 branches each had an independent small-batch layout.

03

Factory Fabrication

Five-axis CNC machined 4,800 ㎡ of Statuario slabs and 1,200 linear metres of Absolute Black counters to a tolerance of ±0.5 mm.

04

Heavy-duty Packing

Absolute Black 30 mm thick slabs and Statuario slabs travelled in steel-framed wooden crates — zero breakage throughout city delivery.

05

Night Installation

Branch works concentrated between 22:00 and 06:00 from Tuesday to Friday, with each branch offline for under 7 days; HQ works scheduled into weekends and non-trading hours.

06

Crystallised Polishing

Statuario slab flooring siliconised and crystallised to a gloss of 90° or above, stain resistance grade 5; gloss still at 86° after three years in service.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — in a financial institution, precision hides in the millimetres and the security envelope.

Anti-static Finish

Trading-floor stone has six-sided conductive backing plus a copper-foil earthing grid; surface resistance 1×10⁶–1×10⁹ Ω, meeting electronic-trading equipment anti-static requirements.

Magnetic Shielding

Vault stone cladding lined with a 0.5 mm titanium-alloy shielding layer; magnetic flux attenuation ≥40 dB, magnetic induction ≤0.5 μT, meeting financial-security standards.

Reinforced Frame

Vault door frame in 80 mm extra-thick stone with a stainless-steel skeleton, 45° mitered and welded corners, overall impact resistance ≥30 kJ.

Night Installation

Branch works always accompanied by a security guard, with work permits and toolboxes checked in and out daily; 52 branches, 18 months, zero security incidents.

Counter Precision

Service counter stone tops flatness ±0.3 mm, counter-partition joint ±0.5 mm; embedded cable trunking pre-laid, transmission lines concealed.

Vault Door Frame

Vault door frame in 80 mm extra-thick stone with a stainless-steel skeleton, pre-embedded with 12 anchors per ㎡, installation accuracy ±0.5 mm, aligned with the surveillance system.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

The strictest financial-security standards, magnetic shielding and 52 branches trading throughout — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers with data.

🔒
CHALLENGE 01

Magnetic shielding and security compliance

Financial-security standards impose strict limits on stone for magnetic, earthing and impact performance; ordinary decorative stone often causes magnetic interference through metal substructures or moisture.

Solution:Vault stone lined with 0.5 mm titanium-alloy shielding plus copper-foil earthing grid; magnetic flux attenuation ≥40 dB, magnetic induction ≤0.5 μT; 80 mm reinforced door frames with 45° mitered welded corners, impact resistance ≥30 kJ.
⚡
CHALLENGE 02

Anti-static trading-floor paving

Trading-floor paving must satisfy anti-static, fire-resistance and wear simultaneously; ordinary stone easily generates static through friction that interferes with electronic equipment.

Solution:Stone with six-sided conductive backing plus copper-foil earthing grid; surface resistance 1×10⁶–1×10⁹ Ω; fire grade A1; Mohs hardness 7; 4,200 ㎡ meets the anti-static requirements of electronic trading equipment.
⏰
CHALLENGE 03

52 branches trading during upgrade

52 branches upgraded in parallel, with each branch offline no longer than 7 days — far shorter than traditional construction cycles.

Solution:Modular factory pre-assembly plus a five-person specialist strike team plus night installation; 52 branches rolled out over 18 months, an average of 5.5 days per branch; zero security incidents, zero customer-service interruptions.
🎯
CHALLENGE 04

±0.5 mm counter and frame precision

Service counter and vault-frame precision directly affects financial-equipment alignment and security grade; the 2–3 mm deviation common with ordinary stone easily causes surveillance failures or security gaps.

Solution:Five-axis CNC machining plus laser-positioned installation; counter top flatness ±0.3 mm, frame corner ±0.5 mm; 1,200 linear metres of counters and 8 vault frames with zero rework.
