Design Concept · A Dignified Yet Welcoming Government Stone Language

The government headquarters embraces the idea of an "open government": the main entrance and civic lobby use book-matched Calacatta Gold feature walls for formal, ceremonial presence; the public service areas and council chamber take Carrara White as their keynote, conveying transparency and openness; the meeting rooms and press briefing hall use contrasting Nero Marquina for depth — three principal stones forming a stone language unique to government, balancing dignity with approachability.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone supplied and installed the 33,000 ㎡ of public-area stone; every stone passed Class A fire rating, seismic intensity 8, stain resistance grade 5 and heavy-metal-free testing. Over 22 months the government tender procedures were followed to the letter — zero violations, zero complaints.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from public tender to phased handover — 22 months of strict tender discipline, every material traceable.

01

3D Scanning

360° laser scanning of the five civic floors produced millimetre-level point clouds, resolving 286 structural deviations in advance.

02

Public Tendering

All five lots were publicly tendered and awarded to the lowest compliant bidder; every material came with certificates of origin and customs declarations.

03

Factory Fabrication

Five-axis CNC machined every slab; Class A fire coatings were factory-applied to a tolerance of ±0.5 mm.

04

Standard Packing

All stone packed to GB/T 18600 with shock-absorbing lining; GPS-monitored transport with zero breakage end to end.

05

On-site Installation

Every worker fully certified — 100% of special trades licensed; every installation traceable to its crew and time.

06

Fire Acceptance

The building's fire coatings passed Class A acceptance first time, with 100% sampling of every joint.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — government work is won in millimetre-level rigour.

Class A Fire Sealing

Every stone joint sealed with Class A fire-rated sealant, fire resistance ≥2 hours once cured, tested to BS 476.

Fire-rated Layering

Stone backed with 30 mm fire-rated rockwool composite giving 4 hours' fire resistance; the whole wall passed Class A acceptance.

Seismic Anchoring

All stone uses underbolt seismic anchors designed to seismic intensity 8, withstanding rare major earthquake loads.

Stone Curtain Wall

Dry-hung curtain wall joints at a standard 1.5 mm width, withstanding 1-in-50-year wind pressure with no risk of dislodgement.

Tamper-proof Seals

Lift Lobbies

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

The strictest tender, fire, seismic and environmental hurdles in government work — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers with data.

📋
CHALLENGE 01

Full tender compliance across 33,000 ㎡

Government tendering is unforgiving — origin certificates, radioactivity reports, customs declarations and social-security records are all mandatory; a single violation voids the entire lot.

Solution: All five lots publicly tendered; every material backed by Italian quarry certificates of origin, customs import declarations and Class A radioactivity testing; dual ISO certification at the factory and site office. 22 months, zero violations, zero complaints.
🔥
CHALLENGE 02

Class A fire rating, 4-hour fire resistance

Fire acceptance is the strictest test for public buildings: all stone must be certified Class A to BS 476 / GB 8624 with fire resistance ≥2 hours.

Solution: Fire coatings factory-applied across the building, with 30 mm fire-rated rockwool composite backing; the whole wall passed a 4-hour BS 476 Part 22 fire test at first acceptance.
📐
CHALLENGE 03

Seismic intensity-8 stone anchoring

Hong Kong's seismic design intensity is 7, with major public buildings designed to 8; curtain-wall stone must never dislodge in a major earthquake.

Solution: The entire building uses underbolt seismic anchoring designed to intensity 8; tested by the national seismological authority to withstand 0.4 g peak acceleration.
🌱
CHALLENGE 04

Heavy-metal-free, environmental commitment

Indoor environmental standards for public buildings are exacting — VOC and heavy-metal emissions must beat E1.

Solution: All stone supplied with EU CE certification, heavy-metal-free test reports and Greenguard Gold certification; after 48 hours of ventilation the indoor air reached E0 grade.
