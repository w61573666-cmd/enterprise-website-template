Design Concept · The Clean Aesthetic of Medical-grade Stone

Union Hospital is conceived as a "garden of recovery": the main entrance and reception lobby take warm Botticino as their feature wall, creating a sense of welcome; the waiting corridors and ward passages use Silver Grey Granite for visual unity, noise reduction and wear resistance; the premium and VIP wards use book-matched Statuario walls for a sense of quality — three stone vocabularies that go beyond cleanliness to give the healing environment a human warmth.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone supplied and installed all public-area stone: 26,000 ㎡ of walls and floors, 12,000 floor pieces, 86 wards and 32 operating-theatre preparation areas, all factory pre-assembled and numbered with five-axis CNC machining to ±0.5 mm; fully enclosed dust-proof hoarding throughout, and in 14 months the hospital never closed or reduced a single bed.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from 3D scanning to dust-controlled construction — 14 months without closing, using full enclosure and zoned isolation for every noisy trade.

01

3D Scanning

360° laser scanning of 32 work zones produced millimetre-level point clouds, resolving 248 structural deviations in advance.

02

Substrate Preparation

Nano-level levelling of wall and floor substrates, with pull-off adhesion tested beyond 0.8 MPa to keep the antimicrobial coating stable long term.

03

Factory Fabrication

Five-axis CNC machined every slab for the 86 wards and 32 operating preparation areas to ±0.5 mm, with all edges rounded to 2 mm.

04

Medical-grade Packing

Every piece wrapped individually in medical-grade dust-free film and shipped in sealed crates to the buffer zone — opened only at the point of installation.

05

On-site Installation

Fully enclosed negative-pressure hoarding with HEPA filtration; workers in protective suits, so no dust escaped into operating areas.

06

Antimicrobial Sealing

Three spray coats of nano-silver antimicrobial coating cured into the stone itself: 99.5% bacterial inhibition, 50,000-cycle wear resistance.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — medical-grade stone lives in every surface patients and staff touch daily.

Antimicrobial Dry-hanging

Ward passages use underbolt dry-hanging with three-way adjustable clamps, themselves antimicrobial-coated, so traditional metal fixings never become a contamination source.

Slip-resistant Flooring

Bush-hammered granite in the waiting corridors, R10 slip resistance UKAS-tested; wheelchairs roll smoothly, never slipping.

Antimicrobial Joints

Ward stone joints filled with nano-silver epoxy; cured joint surfaces inhibit 99.5% of bacteria, meeting operating-theatre cleanliness standards.

Hygienic Drains

Stainless-steel drains set flush into the stone floors of operating preparation areas, with a continuous 1.5° fall and antimicrobial-sealed joints for free-flowing drainage.

Accessible Thresholds

Ward bathroom thresholds use sloped, accessible stone kerbs with rounded arrises, rated to 200 kg — zero barrier for wheelchairs.

Rounded Corners

Every external and internal corner in the wards is rounded to R=10 mm: no right angles to collect dust, easy to clean, meeting infection-control requirements.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

Medical-grade hygiene and uninterrupted operations — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers the four toughest healthcare hurdles with data.

🧫
CHALLENGE 01

Medical-grade antimicrobial performance across 26,000 ㎡

Hospital public areas are chemically disinfected four times a day; ordinary stone corrodes and harbours bacteria. Medical-grade stone needs both antimicrobial and chemical resistance.

Solution: Every slab factory-coated with nano-silver antimicrobial coating, curing into a permanent antimicrobial surface; tested by the Guangdong Institute of Microbiology at 99.5% inhibition, withstanding 5,000 disinfection wipe cycles.
🏥
CHALLENGE 02

14 months of operations uninterrupted

The hospital runs 24/7 — works could not disturb care, and noise, dust and odour had to stay out of the ward zones.

Solution: Fully enclosed negative-pressure hoarding with HEPA filtration and real-time PM2.5 monitoring; noisy trades concentrated on night shifts 22:00–05:00; 14 months with zero complaints and zero infection incidents.
🦠
CHALLENGE 03

Operating-theatre-grade seams and cleanliness

In clean areas such as operating preparation rooms, stone joints must not harbour bacteria — seams must reach operating-theatre grade.

Solution: Heat-fused epoxy joints with nano-silver antimicrobial sealing, joint surface flatness ±0.2 mm; ATP bioluminescence testing showed colony counts below operating-theatre thresholds.
🛏️
CHALLENGE 04

Patient-friendly stone textures

Hospital stone affects patients' emotions; cold tones and sharp edges hinder recovery.

Solution: Warm Botticino with timber-toned sills over light grey granite floors, all external corners rounded; in patient surveys, 87% said "the stone environment helps me relax".
