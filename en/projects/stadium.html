Design Concept · Durability and Ceremony in Stadium Stone

The international stadium is built around "durability and ceremony": 30 mm bush-hammered Steel Grey Granite for the 50,000-seat bowl and public areas delivers durability, Botticino warm stone for the VIP boxes and victory banquets creates ceremony, and mirror-polished Absolute Black Granite for the chairman's platform and athlete tunnel projects refinement — three principal stones balance "outdoor durability" and "indoor ceremony" across the sports architecture.

HSST Stone supplied and installed all stone for the 29,000 ㎡ main bowl: 20,000 ㎡ of tiered seating treads and risers, 5,000 ㎡ of public corridors and athlete tunnel, 3,000 ㎡ of VIP areas and boxes, 1,000 ㎡ of façade and outdoor plaza. Delivered zone by zone over 26 months while the season kept running — 60+ events a year with zero disruption.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from 3D scanning to zoned handover — 26 months, the season kept running.

01

3D Scanning

360° laser scanning of the 50,000-seat bowl and the whole stadium produced millimetre-level point clouds, resolving 526 structural deviations and spectator interface points in advance.

02

Digital Layout

20,000 ㎡ of Steel Grey bush-hammered thick slabs pre-assembled and numbered by seating zone, producing piece-by-piece installation drawings aligned with the zoned works programme.

03

Factory Fabrication

Five-axis CNC machined 20,000 ㎡ of 30 mm bush-hammered granite and 12,000 linear metres of seating caps to a tolerance of ±0.5 mm.

04

Heavy-duty Packing

The seating bowl's 30 mm thick slabs travelled in steel-framed wooden crates — zero breakage throughout city delivery.

05

Seating Installation

A 50-tonne mobile crane with vacuum lifters installed to the zoned programme; night transport avoided event peaks.

06

Weatherproof Sealing

Seating joints sealed with MS Polymer modified silane sealant, UV- and ozone-resistant, no cracking across -40 °C to 90 °C thermal cycles; 50-year design life.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — in a major stadium, precision hides in the millimetres and the seals.

Hidden Bolts

Seating stone is fixed with M12 stainless-steel back-bolts plus three-way adjustable aluminium brackets, capacity ≥8 kN per point; one panel loosening does not affect its neighbours.

Drainage Channel

Seating treads have a 30 mm drainage channel with 12 L/min/m drainage capacity; rainwater cleared within 5 minutes during heavy rain.

Outdoor Protection

Seating stone with six-sided fluorocarbon protective coating plus joint sealant; 5,000-hour accelerated weathering test shows no chalking or discolouration.

Anti-slip Finish

Seating treads with bush-hammered finish and rolled anti-slip grooves, anti-slip grade R11; wet-state coefficient of friction ≥0.68, meeting stadium safety standards.

Seating Installation

Seating tread-riser joint ±0.5 mm; treads have a 30 mm anti-slip nose and a 20 mm drainage channel at the back, overall flatness ±1.5 mm over 3 m.

Corner Detail

Curved seating sections were five-axis CNC profiled, arc-length tolerance ±2 mm; joints sealed with wet-dry separation for double protection against rainwater ingress.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

50,000 spectators in dense foot traffic, 50 years of outdoor durability, and 60+ events a year with construction during the season — HSST Stone answers with data.

🏟️
CHALLENGE 01

Wear resistance for 50,000 spectators

Each event draws 50,000 spectators in and out; ordinary stone easily chips, cracks and dulls under shoe impact and high-frequency foot traffic.

Solution:30 mm bush-hammered Steel Grey Granite with rolled anti-slip grooves, 18 MPa flexural strength, anti-slip grade R11; zero damage during 50,000 spectator turn-outs, ≤0.3 mm wear after 5 years.
🌧️
CHALLENGE 02

50 years of outdoor durability

The seating bowl is 100% outdoor, exposed long-term to UV, acid rain, thermal cycling and freeze-thaw; ordinary stone chalks, discolours and cracks.

Solution:Six-sided fluorocarbon protective coating plus MS Polymer modified silane sealant; 5,000-hour accelerated weathering test shows no chalking or discolouration; freeze-thaw cycles ≥200; 50-year design life.
⏰
CHALLENGE 03

26 months of zoned delivery, season open

The stadium hosted 60+ events a year; works could not affect the season, and noisy trades had to fit into gaps between events.

Solution:Works were zoned across four seating sections, with noisy trades concentrated between 09:00 and 18:00 from Monday to Thursday; spectator corridors fully closed during the season; 60+ events a year ran with zero disruption across all 26 months.
⚡
CHALLENGE 04

Seating drainage and safety

Poor seating-bowl drainage during storms can easily cause spectator slips; ordinary 30 mm seating stone cannot satisfy both load-bearing and drainage.

Solution:Seating treads with a 30 mm drainage channel, 12 L/min/m drainage capacity; a 30 mm anti-slip nose at the front and a 20 mm drainage channel at the back; rainwater cleared within 5 minutes during heavy rain, wet-state coefficient of friction ≥0.68.
