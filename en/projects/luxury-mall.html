Central Luxury Mall · Five-level atrium · 28,000 sqm marble works

Design Concept · A Marble Narrative Above the Atrium

Located in the heart of Central, Hong Kong, the five-level void atrium is the visual and pedestrian hub of the entire mall. HENGSHENG laid 18,000 sqm of Crema Marfil beige marble across all five levels to set a warm keynote; Calacatta Gold wraps the 24 round columns of the atrium, carrying continuous gold veining from the ground floor to the top level; Nero Marquina's black-and-white contrast defines the lift-lobby feature walls with understated luxury after dark.

Because the mall kept trading throughout the day, HENGSHENG delivered the entire chain — quarry selection, slab procurement, factory pre-layout and numbering, through to 100% night-time installation on site: 28,000 sqm of finished stone, 24 curved columns and dust-free dry-laying with full crystallisation, all digitally pre-laid and numbered in the factory for "arrive and install" delivery with zero re-working on site.

Construction Process

Construction Process

The mall kept trading by day, so all works ran from 23:00 to 06:00. The programme advanced through six stages — night survey and setting-out, zone-by-zone shop-drawing layout, factory fabrication and numbering, night hoisting in batches, dust-free dry-laying with same-night grinding, and full crystallisation before opening. Every step carried its own data standards and signed accountability.

01

Night Survey & Setting Out

🌙
Night Survey & Setting Out

The mall trades by day; works ran only 23:00–06:00. 3D scanning built point-cloud models to absorb structural deviations in advance.

With the mall open throughout the day, every survey and setting-out task ran between 23:00 and 06:00; 3D laser scanners built point-cloud models zone by zone, overlaid on the design drawings to resolve structural deviations in advance.

02

Zone-by-Zone Shop-Drawing Layout

📐
Zone-by-Zone Shop-Drawing Layout

Five levels × 4 zones each, 20 work segments in total; factory pre-layout and numbering, piece-by-piece installation drawings.

The five levels were divided into four zones per level — 20 work segments in total — with deepened shop drawings, pre-layout and numbering completed in the factory, outputting piece-by-piece installation drawings so the site picks each slab by number with zero trial and error.

03

Factory Fabrication & Numbering

🏭
Factory Fabrication & Numbering

Slabs cut, sealed and numbered in the factory, packed in reverse installation order for zero on-site re-working.

Slabs were cut, sealed on six faces and numbered piece by piece in the factory, then packed in reverse installation order so they could be laid directly on arrival — avoiding secondary fabrication in the constrained night works.

04

Night Hoisting in Batches

🚚
Night Hoisting in Batches

Service lifts restricted to 01:00–04:00; materials hoisted in batches by work segment, staggered to keep daytime trading untouched.

Service-lift access was limited to 01:00–04:00, so materials moved in batches against a transport plan compiled by work segment — staggered logistics kept the mall fully open every day.

05

Dust-Free Dry-Laying + Same-Night Grinding

💨
Dust-Free Dry-Laying + Same-Night Grinding

Negative-pressure hoarding + low-noise cutting; grinding completed the same night, aisles reopened before the morning opening.

Negative-pressure hoarding and low-noise cutting controlled dust and noise; dry-laying was followed by grinding the same night, with the floor fully reopened before the mall opened the next morning.

06

Full Crystallisation Before Opening

✨
Full Crystallisation Before Opening

Whole-floor crystallisation eliminated lippage; final flatness ±1.5 mm with uniform gloss.

Before the opening milestone the entire floor received unified crystallisation, eliminating lippage at the joints to a final flatness of ±1.5 mm with consistent gloss and tone.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Working inside a trading mall makes dust, noise and safety the first hurdles — alongside colour control across 28,000 sqm and 360° veining continuity on 24 round columns. Negative-pressure hoarding, low-noise cutting and a night-only workflow answered the operational interference, while same-vein blending, factory pre-layout and five-axis CNC curved panels secured the quality. Six key installation details below.

🛡️
Negative-Pressure Hoarding

Working inside a trading mall: hoarding with negative pressure locked dust inside each work segment, keeping public areas clean.

Negative-Pressure Hoarding

Each work segment was enclosed with negative-pressure hoarding, combined with wet working and air filtration to keep dust locked inside the work zone — the mall's daytime air quality was never affected.

🔇
Low-Noise Cutting

Night works used low-noise cutting equipment and damping pads, keeping noise within the mall's permitted range.

Low-Noise Cutting

Night works used low-noise cutting equipment with damping pads, paired with strict working-hour management to keep construction noise within the range acceptable to the mall and its neighbours.

🎨
Same-Vein Blended Laying

28,000 sqm at bulk scale: slabs from 6 batches of one vein blended, with factory pre-layout keeping the tone continuous.

Same-Vein Blended Laying

Colour control across 28,000 sqm was the core challenge; slabs from six batches of the same vein were blended and pre-laid in the factory, keeping the tone of the five-level flooring continuous and consistent.

🏛️
Five-Axis CNC Curved Column Panels

24 round columns machined into curved panels by five-axis CNC, assembled with temporary positioning rings for 360° veining continuity.

Five-Axis CNC Curved Column Panels

The curved panels for the 24 atrium columns were machined piece by piece by five-axis CNC from the 3D model, then calibrated on site with temporary positioning rings — 360° veining continuity with no layer breaks.

👣
Thickened 20 mm Anti-Slip Flooring

High-footfall flooring in thickened 20 mm slabs, surface treated to slip rating R10 — wear resistance and safety together.

Thickened 20 mm Anti-Slip Flooring

For heavy mall traffic the flooring used thickened 20 mm slabs for higher load and wear capacity; the surface was treated to slip rating R10, balancing safety with visual quality.

✨
Nano Protection + Crystallisation

Whole-floor nano protective coating and crystallisation: stain-resistant and easy to clean, cutting the mall's long-term maintenance cost.

Nano Protection + Crystallisation

Before handover the whole floor received a nano protective coating and crystallisation, improving stain resistance and cleanability and significantly reducing the mall's long-term cleaning and maintenance cost.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

Working inside a trading mall, colour control across 28,000 sqm, 360° veining continuity on 24 round columns and wear resistance under heavy footfall form the project's four-fold engineering constraint. HENGSHENG resolved each in turn with negative-pressure hoarding, same-vein blending, five-axis CNC and thickened anti-slip finishing — key metrics and solutions below.

🌙
CHALLENGE 01

Working Inside a Trading Mall

The mall stayed open every day; dust, noise and site safety were the first concerns, and any disruption would hit tenants and footfall.

Solution: Negative-pressure hoarding locked down dust, low-noise cutting controlled noise, the entire workflow ran at night (23:00–06:00), and temporary hoarding with warning systems protected the public.
🎨
CHALLENGE 02

Colour Control Across 28,000 sqm

Supplying five levels in one programme, colour variation between quarry batches would show as visible bands at the joints.

Solution: Slabs from six batches of the same vein were blended, pre-laid and numbered in the factory, then laid to number on site — a continuous, consistent tone across the whole floor.
🏛️
CHALLENGE 03

360° Veining Continuity on 24 Round Columns

The curved panels wrapping the 24 atrium columns had to match vein around every column; a single misplaced panel breaks the whole column's visual.

Solution: Five-axis CNC machined each curved panel from the 3D model; temporary positioning rings calibrated assembly on site, achieving continuous 360° veining with no layer breaks.
👣
CHALLENGE 04

Wear Resistance Under Heavy Footfall

Daily crowds place strict demands on floor wear and slip performance; wear or slip hazards affect operations directly.

Solution: Thickened 20 mm slabs raised load and wear performance, the surface was treated to slip rating R10, and a nano protective agent cut long-term maintenance cost.
