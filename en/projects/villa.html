Design Concept · The Warmth of Stone at Home

The bespoke residence is conceived around its owner's character: the foyer and living room open with light-catching Calacatta Gold book-matched walls, the bedrooms, tea room and study take Carrara White for a tranquil keynote, and the courtyard, exterior walls and terraces choose Roman Travertine to echo the mountain scenery — stone becomes an extension of the owner's personality, not mere decoration.

HSST Stone provides a fully bespoke home service: 3,800 ㎡ of walls and floors, 8 bathrooms, 3 fireplaces and 1 spiral staircase, each home with its own dedicated file; factory pre-assembly and numbering before a single, coordinated delivery, with a dedicated installation crew on site right through to handover.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from site re-measure to butler-style, one-file-per-home delivery — the owner takes part in every detail.

01

Site Measuring

Designer, owner and contractor re-measured on site together; laser scanning produced a 1:1 point cloud, resolving 86 structural deviations.

02

Selection & Layout

A dedicated selection booklet per home; slabs virtually pre-assembled, and cutting began only after the owner confirmed the book-match effect on their phone.

03

Bespoke Fabrication

Fireplaces, the staircase and moulded profiles combined five-axis CNC with hand craft; curved panels machined to ±0.5 mm.

04

Numbered Packing

Crated per home and packed in installation order with foam plus wooden crates — zero breakage throughout city delivery.

05

On-site Installation

A dedicated crew per home stayed on site, coordinating daily with M&E and joinery; the single-residence installation took 18 days.

06

Final Finishing

Full-area grinding and crystallisation followed by room-by-room acceptance: flatness ±0.8 mm and 100% uniform joints.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft details up close — luxury lives in the details an owner touches every day.

Book-matching

The living room's double-height wall uses book-matching: six slabs numbered by veining in the factory, installed so the pattern crosses the joints seamlessly.

Herringbone Laying

The master-bedroom corridor uses herringbone marble; laser setting-out carried the 45° pattern through the whole home at 0.8 mm joints.

Waterjet Medallion

The foyer's 2.4 m medallion was waterjet-cut into 380 pieces, pre-assembled and numbered as a whole, then reassembled on site to the drawing.

Bespoke Fireplaces

Three marble fireplaces CNC-carved as one piece, with heat-resistant stone lining the firebox — seamless, no joints.

Book-match Joints

Book-matched wall joints tinted with colour-matched epoxy; after mirror polishing the veining crosses the joints seamlessly.

Crystallised Polishing

Whole-home stone siliconised and crystallised to stain resistance grade 5; wet areas additionally anti-slip treated to R10 for family safety.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

Personalisation, zero error and butler-style delivery — the three challenges of a bespoke residence, answered with data.

📐
CHALLENGE 01

Millimetre re-measuring for a bespoke home

Structural and M&E deviations compound in a luxury residence, and bespoke pieces such as fireplaces and staircases are extremely dimension-sensitive.

Solution: 3D laser scanning created one file per home; all 86 deviations were resolved at the factory, leaving zero re-cutting on site.
🧱
CHALLENGE 02

Safety of cantilevers and bespoke pieces

Stair treads and fireplace cantilevers carry complex loads, and a family setting demands large safety margins.

Solution: Every bespoke piece comes with structural calculations, fixed with both underbolts and chemical anchors, all verified by a third-party structural review.
🕰️
CHALLENGE 03

Single-residence delivery in 8 months

A single luxury home is schedule-critical, and owners are highly sensitive to the move-in date.

Solution: One dedicated crew per home with daily interface coordination meetings; factory pre-assembly cut the on-site programme by 40%, delivering on time in 8 months.
🌿
CHALLENGE 04

Home-grade environmental and anti-slip standards

Family living demands far higher standards of radioactivity and wet-area slip resistance than public buildings.

Solution: All stone supplied with Class A radioactivity reports; wet areas reach R10 slip resistance; after 48 hours of ventilation, indoor air was tested before handover.
