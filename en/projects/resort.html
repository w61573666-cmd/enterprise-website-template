Maldives Island Resort Renovation Stone Works

19,000 ㎡ · Renovation while trading · Salt-air outdoor durability

Project Overview

Project Overview

Maldives island resort renovation stone works — a full 19,000 ㎡ refurbishment of a 15-year-old resort, covering 240 guestrooms, pool deck, beach walkways, restaurants and bars, spa and exterior rain-screen. Unlike new build, renovation poses a triple challenge: trading without closing, a degraded old substrate, and Indian Ocean salt spray. HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone delivered through 8 rolling phases with no single area closed longer than 30 days — occupancy held above 85% and guest complaints in renovated zones were zero.

Design Concept · Continuity and Upgrade in Renovation

The renovation is built around "continuity and upgrade": guestroom floors and the lobby keep the resort's warm memory with Botticino Classico, restaurants, bars and the spa are upgraded to the mirror feel of Crema Marfil, and the pool deck, beach walkways and exterior rain-screen switch to bush-hammered Roman Travertine — three principal stones balancing "preserved memory" and "overall upgrade" in a renovation project.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone carried out the full stone removal, supply and installation for the 19,000 ㎡: 240 guestrooms refurbished in 8 batches of 30 rooms; 4,500 ㎡ of pool deck and beach walkways replaced in Roman Travertine; and exterior rain-screen cladding installed at height while the resort kept trading. Across the 20-month renovation the resort remained open, average occupancy held above 85%, and renovated zones recorded zero guest complaints.

Construction

Construction Process

Six stages from demolition survey to phased handover — 20 months in 8 rolling phases while the resort kept trading.

01

3D Survey & Assessment

360° laser scanning plus infrared thermography across the 19,000 ㎡ marked 1,286 hollow spots and substrate defects, producing a zone-by-zone renovation plan.

02

Finished-Work Protection

Double acoustic and dust enclosures between trading and works zones; furniture moved through a dedicated wrapped lift route — zero damage claims across all 8 phases.

03

Factory Pre-fabrication

All stone cut, edged and six-side protected in the factory, leaving only dry installation on site; wet trades cut by 90% and noisy works shortened by 40%.

04

Sea-freight Packing

Slabs from Italy and Spain shipped to Male in steel-framed crates with moisture-proof vacuum packing; three shipment batches with zero breakage.

05

Zoned Installation

240 guestrooms refurbished in 8 batches of 30, each within 30 days; outdoor works at night in the dry season without disturbing daytime trading.

06

Crystallised Handover

Indoor stone siliconised and crystallised to a gloss above 90°; each zone handed over after 48 hours of deep clean and ventilation, move-in ready.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Renovation craft up close — salt spray, waterproofing and the old substrate hide in the millimetres.

Floor Movement Joints

Old substrate joints re-cut at 6 m centers and sealed with elastic sealant; not a single hollow-spot callback in the 12 months after renovation.

Pool Coping

Coping pieces with rounded arris and drip channel so overflow drains away; no efflorescence in the salted pool-water environment.

Exterior Rain-screen

Open-jointed rain-screen cladding with a back-ventilated cavity; zero panels lost in force-6 wind-pressure testing through cyclone season.

Salt-air Protection

Outdoor stone six-side fluorocarbon protected with MS-modified silane joint sealant; no chalking or rust staining after 500 hours of salt-spray testing.

Bathroom Waterproofing

Bathroom walls six-side waterproof coated with sealed joints; 48-hour ponding tests on all 240 rooms with zero leakage.

Safety Round-overs

All public-area wall corners and counters rounded to R20; zero guest bump-injury complaints after renovation.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

Renovation while trading, a 15-year-old substrate, and Indian Ocean salt spray — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers with data.

🏨
CHALLENGE 01

Renovation without closing

The resort traded throughout at 85% occupancy; works could not hurt revenue or disturb in-house guests.

Solution:19,000 ㎡ delivered in 8 rolling phases, each area closed at most 30 days; factory pre-fabrication cut on-site noisy works by 40%; revenue grew 6% year on year during renovation with zero guest complaints.
🧱
CHALLENGE 02

A 15-year-old substrate

Old floor substrates were hollow and cracked with flatness deviations up to 12 mm; direct tiling would simply re-hollow.

Solution:Laser scanning marked 1,286 defects; vibration-free milling removed hollow areas and self-levelling brought flatness to ±2 mm; zero hollow-spot callbacks in the 12 months after renovation.
🏖️
CHALLENGE 03

Indian Ocean salt spray

Less than 50 m from the shoreline, salt spray attacks ordinary stone — chalking and efflorescence appear within a year.

Solution:All outdoor areas re-clad in bush-hammered Roman Travertine with six-side fluorocarbon protection and MS-modified silane sealant; no chalking after 500 hours of salt-spray testing and no efflorescence at the first annual inspection.
💧
CHALLENGE 04

Pool waterproofing and slip resistance

The pool deck is permanently wet and the water chlorinated; ordinary stone stains and turns slippery.

Solution:Travertine with six-side waterproof backing and drip-channel coping, anti-slip grade R11, wet-state coefficient of friction ≥0.68; 48-hour ponding tests in all 240 bathrooms with zero leakage.
