Design Concept · The Art of Stone Above a Sail

The project takes the "billowing sail" as its architectural image, and the interiors continue the sea-and-sky motif: the lobby builds a double-height feature wall of gold-veined Calacatta marble, like sunlight scattered across the sea; the guest-room areas take Carrara White for a pure keynote, while the black-and-white drama of Nero Marquina fills the lift lobbies and night-time spaces with the theatricality befitting a seven-star hotel.

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone delivered the entire chain — quarry selection, slab procurement, deep fabrication, pre-assembly and on-site installation: 30,000 ㎡ of finished stone, 200+ shaped columns and over 4,000 curved panels, all digitally pre-laid and numbered in the factory for "arrive and install" delivery with zero re-working on site.

Construction

Construction Process

A six-stage record from survey to handover — every step with its own data standards and signed accountability.

01

Survey & Setting Out

3D laser scanners swept all 30,000 ㎡ zone by zone, generating point clouds overlaid on the design drawings to resolve 237 structural deviations in advance.

02

Digital Layout

Every slab scanned, numbered and virtually pre-assembled in the factory, with piece-by-piece installation drawings — the site picks up each slab by number and installs with zero trial and error.

03

Factory Fabrication

Five-axis CNC and high-pressure waterjet cut the curved panels for 200+ columns and 4,000+ shaped pieces to the millimetre.

04

Numbered Packing

Packed in reverse installation order, crates lined with shock-absorbing foam and humidity cards; 22 days at sea to Dubai with zero breakage.

05

On-site Installation

Vacuum suction working with purpose-made lifting gear; high-level work calibrated slab by slab with laser projection, peaking at 180 ㎡ installed in a single day.

06

Full-area Grinding

After installation the whole area was ground and crystallised to eliminate lippage: final flatness ±1.5 mm, gloss above 90°.

Installation Details

Installation Details

Craft standards up close — the millimetre-level rigour behind the luxury.

Dry-hanging System

Walls use underbolt dry-hanging with three-way adjustable 304 stainless clamps; all concealed works were third-party structurally reviewed.

Large-format Tiling

1,600 mm slab flooring laid with levelling clips to joints under 1.0 mm, double-checked by laser level.

Waterjet Medallion

The lobby's 9 m medallion comprises 1,286 pieces cut to 0.1 mm accuracy; approved as a full pre-assembly before leaving the factory.

Column Wrapping

Curved panels in quarter-circle segments, calibrated one by one with a temporary positioning ring; joint deviation ≤0.3 mm.

Seamless Joints

Book-matched joints tinted with colour-matched epoxy; after arris polishing the gloss matches the slab face — visually seamless.

Crystallised Polishing

Nero Marquina walls siliconised and crystallised to stain resistance grade 5, cutting routine maintenance cost by 40%.

Technical

Technical Highlights & Solutions

The four toughest challenges of the world's tallest hotel — HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED Stone answers with data.

📏
CHALLENGE 01

Flatness control across 30,000 ㎡

Wind-load sway and thermal movement in a super-tall structure make large stone floors prone to waviness and debonding.

Solution: Laser positioning with a digital pre-layout system, plus a levelling datum every 500 ㎡; all slabs factory pre-assembled and numbered, laid to number and ground flat zone by zone.
🏛️
CHALLENGE 02

Fabricating 200+ shaped columns

Columns ranged from 900–1,600 mm in diameter and 4–12 m tall, each curvature different; conventional template work could not converge on tolerance.

Solution: Five-axis CNC machined each panel from the 3D model, assembled on site with temporary positioning rings; every column was trial-assembled in a full circle before dispatch — deviation ≤0.3 mm or it doesn't ship.
🚚
CHALLENGE 03

Ocean freight coupled to the programme

Fabrication, sea freight, customs and installation had to chain together within 14 months; one late batch would lock up the site.

Solution: Eighteen batches shipped in reverse installation order, each with its own crating plan and insurance; a resident coordination engineer made "crate arrives, install begins" a reality.
💧
CHALLENGE 04

Salt-spray attack in a coastal environment

The Arabian Gulf's salt-laden, humid air is a long-term threat to metal fixings and stone protection systems.

Solution: All dry-hanging fixings in 316L stainless steel; stone sealed on six faces with a nano protective agent and salt-spray tested, effective for 10+ years.
