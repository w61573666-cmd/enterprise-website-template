MOQ & Volume Pricing Explained

What is the minimum order? Are small orders treated badly? How big are volume discounts? This page explains our MOQ policy, tier pricing logic and mixed-container rules.

MOQ / Tiered Pricing / Mixed Containers
MOQ Overview

MOQ Policy at a Glance

Slabs: by the piece

From one piece — HK warehouse pickup or delivery

Cut-to-size: from 50㎡

600×600, 800×800 standards at wholesale price from 50㎡

Custom work: per project

Show flats and profiled pieces assessed from drawings — no hard threshold

Trade containers: from 1 box

Mixed stones welcome; container efficiency lowers logistics cost

Why Low MOQ Works

Why We Can Afford Low MOQ

Direct quarry sourcing

First-hand sources in 60+ countries — no middleman markup, small orders still profitable to serve

Own processing base

CNC and 5-axis lines in-house — small custom runs never wait on outsourcing

5,000㎡+ HK stock

Small orders ship from the same stock that serves big projects

25 years of scheduling

Mature production planning slots small orders in without disturbing big deliveries

Mixed Containers

Mixed Container Rules

Multi-stone mixing

Marble, granite, mosaics and moldings in one box — richer product structure

Multi-size pairing

Cut-to-size and finished pieces share a container for maximum utilization

Boxes not full can share space with other orders; freight split by volume

Loading plan preview
