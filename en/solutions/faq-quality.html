Quality Assurance System Explained

What exactly do the 40+ QC checkpoints check? How does sealed-sample acceptance work? Who covers breakage and after-sales? This page presents our quality system from factory to site.

40+ QC Checkpoints / Sealed Samples / After-Sales Pledge
QC Pipeline

The 40+ Checkpoint Pipeline

01

Block selection

Quarry rating, block numbering and vein assessment — substandard blocks never leave the quarry

→
02

Slab scanning

Every slab high-res scanned and archived; tone and texture managed digitally

→
03

Cutting inspection

Length, width, diagonal and flatness fully checked at ±0.5mm tolerance

→
04

In-process checks

Self-check plus cross-check after every process; profiled pieces verified item by item

→
05

Sealing verification

Six-side sealer coverage checked; brand and dosage recorded

→
06

Final pre-packing check

Grading recheck and surface integrity final pass, logged piece by piece
