Pitfalls to Avoid

Development Procurement Pitfalls

Sample/bulk batch mismatch

Bulk goods from a different batch than the sealed sample trigger handover complaints — lock batches in contract

Squeezing thickness

Facade and floor thickness is a safety baseline — never trade it for price

No facade recalculation

Stone curtain walls need fixing-point calculations — skipping them risks safety

No maintenance plan

Without post-handover care, gloss fades within two years and complaints rise

Incomplete specifications

Missing grading and testing requirements in tenders makes bids incomparable

Price Factors

What Drives the Price

Stone mix strategy

Value stones for main areas, luxury stones as accents — controllable blended rate

Framework locking

Annual unit prices locked in framework agreements — budgets hold against market swings

Volume & phases

Whole-estate packages negotiate better than single towers; phased delivery doesn't affect price

Size optimization

Unified sizes raise yield — the fewer non-standard cuts, the lower the blended cost
