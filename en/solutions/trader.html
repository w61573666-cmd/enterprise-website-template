Pitfalls to Avoid

Trading Pitfalls

Color standard not contracted

If grading isn't in the contract, arrival disputes are hard to resolve

Under-specified packing

Insufficient sea-freight packing raises breakage and eats margin

Single-quarry dependence

One source means one outage stops your supply — always have alternates

Unhedged long orders

FX and raw material swings erode margin when prices aren't locked

No loading supervision

Without loading photos and manifests, short-shipment claims have no evidence
