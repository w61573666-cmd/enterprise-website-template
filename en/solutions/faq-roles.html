Four Client Service Channels Explained

Why does one factory run four separate service channels for architects, contractors, developers and traders? Because success looks different for each. This page details every channel's services, delivery standards and how to choose.

Architects / Contractors / Developers / Traders
Why Four Channels

Why Role-Based Service

Success means different things

Designers need design fidelity, contractors need on-time delivery, developers need cost control, traders need margin room — one rigid process cannot serve four goals

Different counterparties

Designers deal in renderings and samples, sites deal in schedules and acceptance, tenders deal in documents, containers deal in shipping papers

Different risk points

Design fears color variance, sites fear delays, developers fear handover complaints, traders fear breakage — each channel carries its own risk playbook

One quality foundation

Whichever channel you use, you get the same factory, the same 68 QC checkpoints and the same quarry resources — different channels, identical quality
