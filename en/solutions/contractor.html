Pitfalls to Avoid

Procurement Pitfalls

Comparing unit prices while ignoring tolerances, unlocked batches causing colour drift, unsequenced deliveries, and skipped sealing leading to efflorescence — the four most common contractor pitfalls and how to avoid them.

Price-only comparisons

Cheap stone exceeds tolerance — uneven joints and rework cost far more than the saving

No batch locking

Unlocked batches across deliveries produce visible color drift and rejected loads

Unsequenced deliveries

Stone arriving out of sync with the program causes double-handling and damage

Skipping sealing

Unsealed stone develops efflorescence and water stains after installation

Acceptance criteria unset

Color grading and tolerance must be in the contract before delivery, not after
