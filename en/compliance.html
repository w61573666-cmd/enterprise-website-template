Compliance Statement

HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED is committed to strict compliance with applicable laws, regulations and international business ethics across all operations.

Last updated: 3 September 2026

1. Our Commitment

Compliance is the cornerstone of our business and applies to international trade, fabrication contracting, agency partnerships and internal management, supported by institutional oversight and training.

2. Laws & Trade

We strictly follow the laws of the Hong Kong SAR, including import/export, trade, customs and business-registration rules, and respect the laws and international trade norms of project jurisdictions.

3. Anti-Bribery & Conflicts of Interest

We uphold fairness and reject improper benefits of any kind. Staff and partners must declare conflicts of interest; obtaining business through improper means is strictly prohibited.

4. Product & Quality Compliance

Every shipment passes our internal multi-stage inspection, and source-quarry and inspection documentation can be provided on request so product information is truthful and traceable.

5. Privacy & Data Protection

We comply with applicable data-protection law, collect client, partner and employee data on a "minimum necessary" basis, and never disclose it without authorisation.

6. Sustainability & Environment

In quarry partnerships and production we follow "extract and restore" practices and green manufacturing, comply with local environmental requirements, and promote waste recycling and low-carbon operations.

7. Whistleblowing

If you suspect any violation, please report it in confidence to hs@hsst.hk. Reports are handled discreetly and followed up within 48 hours.

8. Oversight & Contact

Compliance oversight rests with management. Questions about this statement may be sent to hs@hsst.hk.
