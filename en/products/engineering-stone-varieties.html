Why Granite for Engineering Products

Natural granite is the preferred stone for Hong Kong outdoor public & landscape works: weatherproof and salt-spray resistant, slip-safe, load-bearing and low-maintenance, it stands up to the territory’s hot, rainy and coastal climate. Consistent supply from a single quarry keeps large batches of kerbs, paving, tree pits and street furniture uniform in colour and size across the whole contract.
