Amazon Green — Samples, Technical Data & Applications

A green quartzite from Brazil inspired by the Amazon rainforest. A dense emerald to deep green ground carries flowing lighter green and white veining. The intense, unrestrained colouring is instantly recognisable and used in luxury interiors seeking strong individuality.
