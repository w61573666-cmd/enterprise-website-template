About Engineered Stone

Engineered stone is formed by pressing natural quartz, stone powder and resin at high temperature — free of the fissures and colour variation of natural stone, with consistent texture and stronger stain resistance. Its signature is 'seamless joining': large countertops flow as one, without vexing seam lines. Hengsheng's precision fabrication delivers truly unified customisation.

  • Homogeneous, no natural fissures
  • Seamless joining, one-piece look
  • Stain-resistant, easy to clean
  • Controlled colour, consistent in bulk
