Marble Console Table — Samples, Technical Data & Applications

A slim marble slab on slender metal legs, set against the wall without blocking circulation — the first impression at the door. Depth is typically 350–450mm with bespoke lengths to suit the wall; tops can be a single slab or bookmatched, and the open base keeps cleaning and storage simple. Suited to entrances, corridors, guest rooms and office receptions.
