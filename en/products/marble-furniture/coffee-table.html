Marble Coffee Table

Marble Coffee Table

A marble slab top on a slim metal or solid timber base, with carefully judged proportion and visual lightness. Round, oval and irregular-edged tables can stand alone or be grouped at different heights; polished tops reflect the room while honed or leathered finishes read warmer. Tops are commonly 18-30mm thick with straight, bevelled or ogee edges, and are sealed against everyday drink spills.

Available Surface Finishes

Coffee table tops are usually honed or leathered to resist fingerprints and cup marks; polished tops suit modern designs framed in glass and metal.

Polished — mirror gloss with crisp veining, for tops and interior floors Honed — soft low-sheen with a smooth touch, for tops and walls Leathered — subtle texture, fingerprint resistant, for dining and console tops Antique — lightly aged with vintage character, for courtyards and casual spaces Acid-washed — even fine-sand texture, slip and stain resistant Sealed — penetrating stain and water protection for easy daily care
