Available Surface Finishes

A high-gloss mirror polish is the classic treatment for white marble, giving reflection and translucency; honed surfaces cut reflection and suit large floors, while brushed and leather finishes add tactile depth on walls.

Polished — mirror gloss, sharpest veining, lobbies and walls Honed — soft low-sheen, hotel rooms and residences Leather — micro-textured, slip-resistant, bathrooms and vanities Bush-hammered — even rough grip, outdoor paving and steps Antique — aged patina with softened edges, classical styles Flamed — high-grip thermal finish, façades and plazas

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Does white marble yellow or stain easily? How much does whiteness vary between quarries? How is vein continuity achieved across a large floor? The six questions asked most before specification, answered here.

Does white marble yellow or stain easily?

With six-side sealing, absorption drops sharply and everyday staining is unlikely. Honed or leather finishes reduce water marks; avoid acidic cleaners.

How do you keep veining continuous over large areas?

We run a 3D dry-lay before dispatch, number every slab to the layout drawing, and the site installs by number for book-matched or vein-matched results.

Where does white marble work best?

Polished white suits lobbies and ballrooms; honed suits residential, healthcare and education; leather or bush-hammered suits wet and outdoor areas.

What maintenance does it need?

Clean with pH-neutral detergent and a soft cloth. Re-seal or re-crystallise every 12–24 months to restore sheen and stain resistance.
