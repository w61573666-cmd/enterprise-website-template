FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How thin can a shaped component be made? What is the minimum radius for curved bending? How is wastage calculated for an inlay pattern? The questions asked most before a bespoke order is placed, answered here.

What tolerance can you hold?

5-axis CNC holds ±0.1mm and waterjet about ±0.2mm; complex relief is hand-finished by senior craftsmen.

Can I supply just one rendering?

Yes. Send a rendering or sketch and we will convert it into a 3D model and shop drawings for approval.

What is the lead time?

Typically 4–8 weeks depending on complexity; allow 10–12 weeks for large or highly complex pieces.

Will shaped pieces break in transit?

We use purpose-built crates with shock absorption and corner protection; large pieces travel by dedicated vehicle with on-site handling.
