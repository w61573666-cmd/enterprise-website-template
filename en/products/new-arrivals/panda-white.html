Panda White Marble — Samples, Technical Data & Applications

A black-veined white marble from Sichuan. A pure white ground laced with lively grey-black veins like a panda's markings — strong yet harmonious contrast, a recent designer favourite for feature walls and bathrooms. Fine-grained and mirror-polished, best for accent areas and high-end custom work.
