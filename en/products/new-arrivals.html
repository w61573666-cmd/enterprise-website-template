FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Will a new arrival be discontinued soon? Is the 50 m² trial minimum firm? How is colour variation between sample and shipment controlled? The questions asked most before ordering, answered here.

Can I request samples?

Yes. Request up to 3–5 free physical samples online per order; they ship within about 3 working days.

What is the minimum order for new arrivals?

Trial orders start from around 50 m²; once in regular production the MOQ is typically 100–300 m² by variety.

Will supply continue later?

Year one is a trial period, so reserve early. Strong performers move to annual agreements and become regular lines.

How do I hear about new arrivals first?

Follow the New Arrivals section or ask a consultant to join the notification list — we alert you as soon as new stock lands.
