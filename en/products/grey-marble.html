FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we are asked most before specification — answered here first.

Which styles suit grey marble?

Grey is the backbone of modern minimal, industrial, Neo-Chinese and corporate interiors, pairing easily with timber, metal and glass.

Dark or light grey?

Use light grey in small or low-light spaces to avoid heaviness; reserve dark or strongly veined greys for lobbies and feature walls.

Can grey marble be thinned or composited?

Yes. Its stable structure suits 12–15mm engineering panels and aluminium honeycomb composites, cutting façade dead load.

How reliable is supply?

Core greys are covered by annual direct-purchase agreements, so volume can be locked for multi-year projects.
