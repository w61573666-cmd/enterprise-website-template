FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions we are asked most before specification — answered here first.

Granite or marble?

Choose granite for wear resistance, weatherability and low upkeep (outdoors, heavy traffic, kitchen counters); choose marble for artistic veining and luxury (lobbies, feature walls, bathrooms).

Does granite stain?

Granite is dense with absorption usually ≤0.4%, so it resists staining far better than marble, especially after sealing.

Best finish for outdoor paving?

Flamed, bush-hammered or chiselled for grip and wear — flamed for busy plazas, bush-hammered for ramps and steps.

Is granite good for countertops?

Yes — hard, scratch and heat resistant, and a mainstream kitchen choice. Dark varieties should be sealed periodically.
