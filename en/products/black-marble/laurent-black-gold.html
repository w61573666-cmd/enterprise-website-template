Laurent Black Gold — Samples, Technical Data & Applications

A black marble in the same family as Portoro. A pure jet-black ground carries bold flowing white and warm gold veins, creating high contrast and a sense of movement. Used in modern luxury interiors and statement hospitality venues that call for strong visual impact.
