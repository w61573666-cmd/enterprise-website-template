Classic Beige Travertine — Samples, Technical Data & Applications

The highest-volume travertine export from Denizli, Turkey. An even pale beige with fine, uniform voids and high yield, it is among the best-value choices for cladding, interior floors and feature walls. Resin-filled and polished it rivals marble flatness; honed it keeps a rustic natural feel, used heavily in both projects and homes.
