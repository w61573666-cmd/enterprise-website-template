International Authoritative Credentials

Six Major Certifications · End-to-End Traceability

From quarry exploration to project delivery, every step is validated against the corresponding international standard. The following are the authoritative credentials Hengsheng has obtained across its full product line. Original scans, annual surveillance reports and verification numbers are available upon request.

ISO 9001

Quality Management System

End-to-end quality control covering quarry input, processing, finished-product inspection and outbound acceptance. Certifying bodies include BSI / SGS; annual surveillance audits during the validity period.

  • Scope: Quality Management System
  • Coverage: Full Company Operations
ISO 14001

Environmental Management System

Quarry-to-factory environmental management: dust, wastewater, waste and energy consumption are quantified and controlled under the ISO 14001 framework, with annual third-party monitoring and system audits.

  • Scope: Environmental Management System
  • Coverage: Quarries + Processing Bases + Logistics
CE

EU Construction Compliance

Full compliance with the EU Construction Products Regulation (CPR). Physical performance, chemical release and mechanical strength are verified against EN standards — the mandatory entry credential for the EU market and major European-funded projects.

  • Scope: Mandatory EU Market Requirement
  • Coverage: All EU-bound Batches
GREENGUARD Gold

Low-VOC Gold Certification

UL GREENGUARD Gold is one of the strictest indoor-air-quality certifications globally, ensuring stone VOC emissions stay within health-protective thresholds for sensitive spaces such as schools, hospitals and offices.

  • Scope: Indoor Air Quality
  • Coverage: All Indoor-Application Products
NSF/ANSI 51

Food-Contact Material Safety

For kitchen counters, dining counters and food-prep surfaces. NSF tests heavy-metal migration, leachables and surface microbiological indicators per ANSI 51 before issuance.

  • Scope: Food-Service Scenarios
  • Coverage: Full Counter / Dining-Counter Stone Line
ISO 45001

Occupational Health & Safety

Covers occupational health and safety at quarries and processing bases: annual occupational health check-ups, ongoing PPE iteration and emergency drills safeguard every team member worldwide.

  • Scope: Occupational Safety
  • Coverage: All Processing Stages

FAQ

Certification FAQ

QCan you provide original certificates or stamped copies?

For tenders and compliance filings we provide stamped copies; originals are available for on-site inspection or video verification. Every certificate can be authenticated on the issuing body's website by number.

QWhat is the practical value of GREENGUARD Gold for my project?

GREENGUARD Gold certifies low indoor emissions — critical for schools, healthcare, hotel guestrooms and other sensitive spaces. LEED and WELL projects can claim indoor-air-quality credits directly with the certificate.

QWhich applications does NSF/ANSI 51 cover?

NSF/ANSI 51 is the food-contact material safety certification, covering all stone surfaces that touch food directly — kitchen counters, bars and dining tops — citable for hotel F&B areas and premium residential kitchens.

QHow are surveillance audits and validity maintained?

The three ISO systems undergo annual surveillance audits with three-year recertification; CE and GREENGUARD Gold are reviewed yearly. A dedicated owner tracks the audit calendar — coverage has never lapsed.
