Certifications & Honors
Building Long-Term Trust on International Standards

Every certificate, every accreditation, every honor is a milestone answer after 27+ years of focused natural-stone work at Hengsheng. We build our compliance framework around global authorities — ISO, CE, GREENGUARD, NSF/ANSI — covering quarrying, processing and project delivery as a fully traceable system. These credentials are not decorations; they are the entry tickets into 4,200+ long-term project partnerships worldwide.

International Authoritative Credentials

Six Major Certifications · End-to-End Traceability

From quarry exploration to project delivery, every step is validated against the corresponding international standard. The following are the authoritative credentials Hengsheng has obtained across its full product line. Original scans, annual surveillance reports and verification numbers are available upon request.

ISO 9001

Quality Management System

End-to-end quality control covering quarry input, processing, finished-product inspection and outbound acceptance. Certifying bodies include BSI / SGS; annual surveillance audits during the validity period.

  • Scope: Quality Management System
  • Coverage: Full Company Operations
ISO 14001

Environmental Management System

Quarry-to-factory environmental management: dust, wastewater, waste and energy consumption are quantified and controlled under the ISO 14001 framework, with annual third-party monitoring and system audits.

  • Scope: Environmental Management System
  • Coverage: Quarries + Processing Bases + Logistics
CE

EU Construction Compliance

Full compliance with the EU Construction Products Regulation (CPR). Physical performance, chemical release and mechanical strength are verified against EN standards — the mandatory entry credential for the EU market and major European-funded projects.

  • Scope: Mandatory EU Market Requirement
  • Coverage: All EU-bound Batches
GREENGUARD Gold

Low-VOC Gold Certification

UL GREENGUARD Gold is one of the strictest indoor-air-quality certifications globally, ensuring stone VOC emissions stay within health-protective thresholds for sensitive spaces such as schools, hospitals and offices.

  • Scope: Indoor Air Quality
  • Coverage: All Indoor-Application Products
NSF/ANSI 51

Food-Contact Material Safety

For kitchen counters, dining counters and food-prep surfaces. NSF tests heavy-metal migration, leachables and surface microbiological indicators per ANSI 51 before issuance.

  • Scope: Food-Service Scenarios
  • Coverage: Full Counter / Dining-Counter Stone Line
ISO 45001

Occupational Health & Safety

Covers occupational health and safety at quarries and processing bases: annual occupational health check-ups, ongoing PPE iteration and emergency drills safeguard every team member worldwide.

  • Scope: Occupational Safety
  • Coverage: All Processing Stages
Industry Standing

Industry Associations · Strategic Partnerships · Media Recognition

We have built long-term cooperation with leading global bodies in architectural design, materials engineering and geological sciences. The list below highlights representative partnerships and media recognition; the complete strategic-partnership roster is available on the Partnerships page.

Carrara Quarries, Italy

Signed a 10-year exclusive strategic agreement with Carrara Marmi S.p.A. (one of Italy's largest marble-mining groups in the Carrara region) for an annual supply of 50,000 m³ of premium Carrara white marble blocks — locking in the world's finest white marble resources at the source.

China Stone Material Association

A council-member company of the China Stone Material Association, Hengsheng participates deeply in industry standards, quality-classification systems and industrial-policy research, jointly promoting natural stone adoption in green buildings.

Hong Kong Institute of Architects

A long-term partner providing local registered architects with stone samples, technical parameters, processing know-how and project casework — supporting material selection and detail decisions during the building-design phase.

Hong Kong Green Building Council

Active in the Council's green-building-material assessment and case-study research. Multiple Hengsheng projects have been included in the Council's annual case library, providing empirical evidence for local green-building ratings.

Ministry of Natural Resources Key Laboratory

Industry–academia–research collaboration with a key laboratory under China's Ministry of Natural Resources — joint research on stone radionuclide limits, weathering mechanisms and sustainable mining models.

Leading International Architecture Schools

Joint research and scholarship programs with Politecnico di Milano, Tsinghua's School of Architecture, and HKU's Department of Architecture — investing in next-generation architectural-design talent.
