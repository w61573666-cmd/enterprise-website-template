About Us

Global Leader in Premium Marble Technology

Headquartered in Hong Kong and founded in 1999, HENGSHENG MARBLE S&T CO. LIMITED has spent 27+ years focused on global sourcing, smart fabrication and custom engineering of natural stone. Our service network covers 60+ countries with more than 4,200+ projects delivered, including 1,000+ star-rated and premium hotels. Our long-standing client base spans design firms, developers, general contractors and international hotel management groups.

The company operates a sales and showroom centre in Hong Kong and its own smart manufacturing plant in Fujian, China, equipped with Italian five-axis CNC, bridge saws, ultra-high-pressure waterjets and fully automated polishing lines. A quality-control framework of 68 inspection nodes covers every critical step — quarry inspection, slab grading, dimension fabrication and protective treatment — making quality measurable and traceable end to end.

We maintain long-term partnerships with 180+ certified quarries across Italy, Spain, Türkiye, Greece, Brazil, India, Iran, South Africa, Pakistan, Mexico, Egypt and China, covering 300+ mainstream stone varieties. A ten-year exclusive strategic partnership with the Carrara quarry region in Italy secures a stable supply of top-tier white and beige marble for long-term and custom projects.

To Hengsheng, stone is not merely part of a façade or interior finish — it carries the character of a city and the experience of living in it. In every material selection, every process refinement and every on-site collaboration, we strive to balance technical precision with human care, allowing the material to truly join the building's life cycle rather than being a one-off stylistic choice.

Core Technology

Artisan Precision · Tech-Driven

At Hengsheng, technology is not just an accumulation of equipment — it is a complete methodology from raw block to final presentation. By combining digital fabrication, nano-protection and intelligent inspection, we bring the uncontrollable variables of natural stone under tighter management, so it performs consistently over years of use.

PRECISION MACHINING

5-Axis CNC Machining Center

Italian-imported five-axis CNC machines cut and engrave curved profiles, 3D components and large-format marquetry with specialist tooling and dedicated programs, holding tolerances within 0.1mm. In practice this means tighter staircase trims, smoother columns and mouldings, and far less on-site adjustment during installation.

NANO PROTECTION

Nano Sealing Protection

For different stone types and applications we select matched penetrating protectors that penetrate the micro-pores to form a transparent protective layer — improving resistance to water-, oil- and some acid-based staining without visibly changing colour or gloss. Verified by third-party testing, it reduces residue from tea, wine and oil marks and extends maintenance intervals.

SMART QUALITY INSPECTION

Smart Quality Inspection System

Combining our proprietary vision-inspection system with human review, every slab is graded for surface defects, hairline cracks, colour difference and dimensions. Each batch receives a traceable quality record for project teams to consult during acceptance and later maintenance — making stone quality a set of clear, comparable data rather than a matter of feel.
