Corporate Values & Communication Principles

🗣️ Communication Philosophy

Great collaboration begins with clear, sincere communication. From the first material inquiry to detailed design, production scheduling and on-site coordination, we keep you informed at every step — predictable and traceable.

  • Integrity first — every quote, sample and delivery promise is honoured; nothing is hidden or overstated.
  • Open and transparent — quarry sources, processing methods and quality standards are fully disclosed and open to third-party inspection.
  • Fast response — commercial enquiries are answered within 24 hours; urgent matters are escalated directly to a named officer.
  • Professional co-creation — senior stone consultants join the whole process to help designers and contractors choose and apply stone correctly.

We communicate in Cantonese, Mandarin and English; for international projects we can also arrange local-language support so nothing is lost in translation.

📡 Channels & Mechanism

  • Tiered response — standard enquiries within 24h; urgent matters (missing material, colour confirmation, site issues) within 4h.
  • Complaints reach management — hs@hsst.hk is read by management; a solution is proposed within 48h and tracked to closure.
  • Documented commitments — samples, quotations and mill reports are delivered as formal documents for full traceability.
  • Holiday duty — an emergency channel stays open on public holidays so critical milestones are never delayed.

We recommend email for formal project talks (clear record) and WhatsApp / WeChat for day-to-day selection and site coordination (fastest).

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

📦 Can samples be shipped overseas?

Yes. Samples are dispatched within 3 working days after confirmation; international delivery typically takes 5–10 working days. Shipping is charged at cost and credited on your first order.

💰 Is there a minimum order?

Minimums vary by stone and specification: ready-stock slabs allow flexible quantities, while bulk and project orders are quoted separately. Ask us for details.

🎨 How do you handle colour variation?

Natural stone varies by nature. We control variation with batch management, provide batch photos and sample comparisons before shipment, and recommend on-site approval for large projects.

⏱️ How long do orders take?

Ready stock: 7–15 working days. Custom fabrication: 2–6 weeks depending on complexity. Large projects follow a schedule agreed in writing.

🛠️ Do you offer installation?

We provide professional technical support and can recommend experienced Hong Kong installation teams for large projects. Let us know in your inquiry.

🏢 What do you need for a quote?

Any of the following speeds things up: stone type/series, location and area, desired thickness and finish, delivery location and date. We will also follow up if anything is missing.
